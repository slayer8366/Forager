## What to build (B, C, D, F and G of the pi-06 study; not A)

- **B. Downloads on the card.**
  - `curl_resume` downloads into `/mnt/archive/forager-build/sources-osm/`, with a symlink at the
    same name in `sources/osm/`, as today. The `.md5` stays beside the link.
  - Before any download, a check that `/mnt/archive` is a mounted filesystem (`mountpoint -q`)
    with room. If either fails, `die` with a message.
  - **The old-extract cleanup must remove the card file as well as the link.** Today it removes
    only the link, which at 18.8 GB a week would fill the card in about 5 weeks.
- **C. The merged extract on the card.** `$merged` is written under
  `/mnt/archive/forager-build/work/<run_id>/`, and removed after Planetiler and in
  `cleanup_scratch` on failure. `--basemap` runs never create it.
- **D. A byte-based disk check.**
  - Replace the fixed `USCA_MIN_FREE_GB` check with a need computed in bytes from the inputs' sizes.
  - Use the ratios measured on the 261017 run, pinned in `pins.env` with their source:
    - features 5.55×;
    - archive 1.81×;
    - multipolygon about 0.15×;
    - plus anything still on the NVMe;
    - a margin, stated.
  - Run it at the state stage, using Content-Length from Geofabrik's HEAD for downloads, or the
    files' sizes for `--local`, so an over-size week fails before a day of downloading. Run it again
    at the merge.
  - Check both disks: the NVMe for Planetiler, the card for B and C.
  - **Fix the unit slip:** `df -BG` is GiB, but the message says GB. Use bytes, and state the unit.
- **F. Download restarts.**
  - A restart loop for slow or stalled downloads: `--speed-limit` and `--speed-time`, a short pause,
    resume with `-C -`, and a restart cap.
  - The floor must suit the link the Pi has. The Bluetooth tether runs about 0.2 MB/s, so a 700 kB/s
    floor would abort every 40 s. Propose the numbers in Phase 1, from the logs, and pin them in
    `pins.env` with the reasoning.
  - Whether curl's own `--retry` resumes with `-C -` is not verified. Find out from curl's
    documentation or a local test against a small local HTTP server, not Geofabrik.
- **G. Wait for the network.** Before the state stage, wait (bounded) until
  `download.geofabrik.de` resolves and the default route is up. On timeout, `die` with what was
  missing. `--local` runs skip this.
