## The planner's guess (unconfirmed, may be wrong)

The repaint runs in the `setStyle` callback. On MapLibre Native, the sprite's images may not be loaded yet at that point, so `Style.getImage` returns null for every name. If so, Trail Map's night icons have never worked on a device; Sorrel found no record they had ever been seen on one. Other explanations to rule in or out:
- the image names don't match the v5 index;
- `getImage` doesn't return sprite images at all on Native;
- the night switch reloads the style and loses the images;
- the log line counts wrongly.
