## The questions

1. **Topo in the app today.** What the Topographical basemap is now (source, raster or vector, night mode, offline download), and what the own Street map is (vector tiles from the Worker). Name the map library's version and what it supports for contours (vector lines) and hill shading (a hillshade layer from elevation tiles, or pre-rendered shading). Cite file and line at pre-main.
2. **Ways to build it.** At least: contours as vector tiles (lines with elevation, index lines every fifth) in their own PMTiles or inside the map's; hill shading as elevation tiles the phone shades itself, or as pre-shaded image tiles. For each: the tools (named, pinned versions, licences), whether they fit the Pi's existing pipeline on pi-build, and its memory and time.
3. **Sizes, computed and marked so.** For the US and Canada at sensible zoom ranges: contours at two intervals (for example 40 ft and 20 m, given the app's units setting), and shading in each form. Also the extra size per offline area at the app's default and maximum radius. Use published figures or computed estimates, never a download.
4. **Shared with routing elevation.** Can the one 3DEP download Juniper sized for routing (1 arc-second, about 128 GB) feed contours and shading too? Is 1 arc-second fine enough for contours at the zooms people forage at, or does the finer 1/3 arc-second matter, and at what extra size? The Canadian provenance caveat carries over: say how each option handles it.
5. **Disk and time on the Pi,** for a first build and for keeping it current. Does elevation change rarely enough to build once rather than weekly?
6. **What Sage would need to decide:** the contour interval by zoom, colours, index lines and labels, shading strength, night mode, and how it sits under trails and names. Questions only; Juniper designs nothing.
7. **Credits.** What the elevation sources and tools require the app to show, quoted at source.
