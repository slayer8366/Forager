**The planner's reading, a premise for Phase 1 to check.** A turn is a place where the route leaves the way it is on for another way, at a junction or a fork, with a change of direction large enough to be a turn. These are never turns:
- a bend within one way, however sharp;
- carrying straight on through a junction where another way joins.
The distance to it is measured along the route from the walker's place on it.
