## The feature being planned (owner's design, RECORD -963)

A queue in Forager that shares finds, one at a time, to iNaturalist's own Android app:
1. The user selects several finds in the Journal (or opens a find from the map in the Journal) and taps "Share to iNaturalist".
2. Forager shows each find for review and editing, with an "Include location" switch that starts on.
3. On confirm, Forager hands the find's photos (and location, if included) to iNaturalist's app. The user posts there and comes back to Forager for the next find.

Forager's own camera strips location from its saved photos on purpose. Including location would mean Forager writing it into the copies it shares, or passing it some other way.
