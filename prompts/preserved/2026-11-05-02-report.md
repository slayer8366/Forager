## 1. Mechanism prediction, written before I read the diff

From the dispatch's own vocabulary I predicted the promotion would be mostly: a self-hosted vector basemap (the Trail Map) replacing or joining the earlier rasters, with a chip row to choose between them; a trail-following return path; a species-availability screen; copy changes; and a very large volume of non-app lines. I predicted that if it failed, it would fail at the basemap's offline or unreachable state, at a setting not surviving a restart, at Back out of a new screen, at 80% chrome on new chips, or at public text drifting from the app.

What held: the bulk is map-style and tile-server data (309k and 155k lines); the chip row exists and is where most of the recent owner rulings landed; the return path and the Trail Map's fallback states are the largest behaviour changes; the restart-persistence hint was real (the intermittent test). What did not hold: "Availability" is the app's long-standing main screen, not a new one; the Satellite basemap was already gone on main (its own dispatch, -708), so the README line this promotion corrects was stale on main, not a behaviour change here. I had not predicted the volume of navigation work (the way home, the waypoint route along trails, the drop-off stack, route plotting) that makes up most of the 299 app files.
