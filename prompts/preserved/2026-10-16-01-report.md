### Verify findings
- **READ, a correction to the dispatch's premise.** At `eec76f4e` the sequencer did start attempt 3 after the refused attempt 2 (`PinProbe.kt`, the loop and `runOne`). An attempt's line is only logged when the attempt returns. So "never ran attempt 3" is more precisely "attempt 3 started and never returned".
- **READ.** Two waits in `PlatformPinCamera` had no limit: the camera open and the session configure. Every other wait was already bounded. Both now have a 5 s limit.
- **COULD NOT DETERMINE: which wait hung on the S26.** `pin.jsonl` is a single logcat snapshot, and nothing inside an attempt is logged until it returns.
- **COULD NOT DETERMINE: whether the main thread was blocked** in a synchronous camera-service call. Coroutine timeouts cannot rescue that.
- **COULD NOT DETERMINE: the state of the viewfinder.** A screenshot about 5 s after that snapshot shows a viewfinder image, but whether it was live or frozen cannot be told from it.
- **INFERRED, not verified (I did not read the platform source this session).** A refused `openCamera` may also post `onDisconnected` or `onError` afterwards. The code handles both.
- **READ, S26 dumpsys.**
  - The pin log lists `getCameraIdList` as `[0,1,2,3]`, and logical camera 0 has physical cameras 2, 5, 6 and 7.
  - The HAL also has logical devices 20, 21, 23 and 24 that are not in the list, so they cannot be opened directly.
  - Only the first lines of the S22 dumpsys were read (four camera devices; IDs 0, 1 and 3 visible to the old camera API). My parser did not handle its format, and nothing in the build depends on it.

### Decisions made beyond the dispatch (for the planner to accept or reject)
- **Verdicts:** besides the four named ones, I added `timed_out`, `failed` (an attempt that threw before reaching the camera) and `not_attempted` (a planned step not made), so nothing is forced into the four.
- **A session the camera would not configure** is logged as `refused`, with `stoppedAt: configure`.
- **When the session query says no**, the session is not configured at all; the verdict is `unsupported`.
- **Pairs:** every pair of a listed logical camera's back physical cameras. A pair is made only when both share a RAW size within the 16 MP operating limit, because that identical-size pair is all the platform guarantees. Other pairs are logged `not_attempted` with both cameras' sizes. A pair takes one frame only.
- **Direct opens:** made only for listed IDs, as the dispatch says. Unlisted IDs (5, 6 and 7 on the S26) are logged `not_attempted`, not tried.
- **Front cameras** are listed in the enumeration but not attempted.
- **Roles** come from the field-of-view ratio against the main wide.
- **A camera that offers no RAW size** is reached with a preview stream bound to it, and logged `reachable_no_raw`.
- **Reuse:** the reach probe uses the pin probe's own borrow and hand-back.
- **Small edits to existing code:** `SensorWatchLines.JsonLine` was made internal so the reach lines are written the same way, and `PinProbe`'s constructor now takes `log` last.
