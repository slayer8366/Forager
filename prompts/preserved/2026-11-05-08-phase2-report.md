### Decided beyond the dispatch (to accept or reject)
1. "Covered" means inside the downloaded rectangle, not a circle; a circle test would start up to 1.41 times larger.
2. The pin is the middle of the line's north-south and east-west extent.
3. A note hides once the pin or the radius moves, since it's then about a different area. I recommend this.
4. A successful download from the trip's page ends the trip, so leaving afterwards doesn't put back the earlier entry.
5. Leaving during a running download puts the page back at once.
6. A second trip tapped before leaving keeps the first tap's "before".
7. A route's radius is held as the user's own, so a change of units doesn't reset it.
8. The shared test screen now wires the icon the way MainActivity does.
