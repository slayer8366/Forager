## Three things that change the plan
1. **On the card, the kind word is not in a fitted status line. It is the left part's own third line.** [read] It is drawn at `NavigationCardParts.kt:171-172` (`DistanceKindText`, :176-189). The card calls `statusLineFit` with `kindInStatus = false` (`NavigationCardLines.kt:144`), so `kindLead` is always null in production. The only code that sets `kindInStatus = true` is `firstRowFit` (`NavigationHudFit.kt:70-96`), and it has no production caller; git grep finds only `NavigationHudFitTest.kt:77-98`. So "By trail · Approaching" cannot appear on today's card. The change belongs in `LeftPart` and the fit's width sum, not in `statusLineFit`.
2. **Strict halves need roughly twice the wider item.** [inferred, estimates below] At font 1.0 a straight "≈ 1250 ft" with an evening line doesn't fit its half on a 360 dp phone. So the large-text fallback (Q3) will show at normal text size on common phones, not only at font 2.0.
3. **Wherever the left part sets the card's height, the card loses one line.** [inferred] The card is as tall as the taller of its two parts (`NavigationCardParts.kt:79`). With one evening piece, as in your screenshots ("Dark since 5:23 PM"), the left part is the taller. That follows from the owner's words, so it is not an option. It does mean the landscape evening-line decisions (-955 C, D42) may now keep the lines in more cases, never fewer. Two tests pin equal heights (see 5).

## 5. Options for the owner (the owner's words are quoted in each)

Owner, verbatim: "For navigation cards have the 2.2 mi on the left half, and then move the "by trail" from the bottom to the top right half, on the same line as 2.2 mi", "And do the same for landscape", "Keep the text size the same size".

**Q1. How do "2.2 mi" and "by trail" share the top line?**
- **A. Each centred in its own half.**
  ```
  |   2.2 mi     by trail   | Dark since 5:23 PM  X
  |  left 74° ▶ ◀ 206° SW   |
  ```
- **B. Meeting at the middle, read as one phrase "2.2 mi by trail" (Recommended).**
  ```
  |      2.2 mi by trail    | Dark since 5:23 PM  X
  |  left 74° ▶ ◀ 206° SW   |
  ```
  The distance ends at the middle and "by trail" starts there. It reads as the owner's own -656 phrase, "0.4 mi by trail". With A, the gap (about 50 dp on the S22) can make "by trail" read as a label for the heading under it.
- **C. One centred phrase.** It needs the least room, but a short figure crosses into the right half, which bends "left half".
- In every option the two sit on one baseline, like words in one line of text.

**Q2. When there is no "by trail" or "straight" ("Arrived", or a dash with no fix or no route yet), where does that word sit?**
- **a. Centred across the left part, as today (Recommended).** The word only moves when it changes anyway (a number becomes "Arrived" or "—"). A lone word in the left half beside an empty right half looks like something is missing.
- **b. In the left half, with the right half empty.**

**Q3. Where the two can't share the line at today's size (a long figure, a narrow phone, large text), what moves?**
- **a. "by trail" drops to its own line just under the distance, above the turn line (Recommended).**
  ```
  |   ≈ 1250 ft   |
  |   straight    |
  | 169° ▶ ◀ 206° SW |
  ```
  The distance and what it measures stay together. It's decided for the longest distance the card could show, so it doesn't jump between lines as the number changes while walking.
- **b. "by trail" goes back to its old place at the bottom.** It is separated from the distance again, which is what the owner just moved it away from.
- **c. The evening times beside it narrow first** ("Dark / since / 5:23 / PM"), and the kind drops only if that isn't enough. The card gets taller. In landscape that can push the evening times off the card altogether (-955 C), which a user could read as the sunset time having gone missing.
