## A5, plainly: can a torn read decode as wrong-but-valid data here?
- **The drop-off's map tiles: practically no.**
  - 972 of the 973 basemap rows in the end-s14 copy are zlib-compressed. `StoredTiles.bytesOf` inflates them to the end, where zlib checks the Adler-32 of the whole tile, so a torn tile fails safe.
  - [observed, the laptop's zlib] 0 of 3,897 page-sized splices of real stored tiles were accepted.
  - **A premise to correct:** the check is zlib's Adler-32, not "gzip's check". Gzip applies only to a body still gzip-wrapped, and there are none in that copy.
- **A raw, uncompressed vector-tile row: yes, it can.**
  - `MvtDecoder` checks structure but not coordinates (`:95-96`), so a foreign span inside a geometry decodes as wrong points [inferred from the code].
  - One such row exists in the copy: 73 bytes, inside one page.
- **The waypoint bubble's relief PNGs: could not determine.**
  - 20 of the 44 relief rows are raw PNGs of about 150 KB each.
  - Whether a torn one fails safe depends on the phone's image decoder, which was not read.
  - If it accepts a damaged image, the bubble could show a wrong height.
  - This is the one place where the inference in the owner's option ("would very likely produce data the app can't make sense of") is not established. Reported, not built on.

## Calls beyond the dispatch, for you to accept or reject
1. **Retry scope.** The whole read is retried, the open and its statements together: I read B1's "from a read-only open" as the connection. SQLite checks for a hot journal at every statement that starts a read, and on the JVM the refusal comes at the first query.
2. **No AppContainer change.** The log sink and pause come through constructor defaults (`Log.w` under each reader's tag, and `Thread.sleep`), so `AppContainer.kt` is untouched.
3. **The waits block the IO thread** for up to 750 ms per read.
   - `MapLibreOfflineBasemap` holds its lock through them, so other reads queue behind a retrying one.
   - A store that never clears costs Navigate about 1.5 s: its two ends are read in turn.
4. **Evidence read beyond the code.** I copied the end-s14 `mbgl-offline.db` to scratch and queried only the copy: `cmp` identical, sha256 prefix bc0976616ecf698a. I also read Robolectric's Android 16 framework jar with `javap`.
5. **How the reverts were made.** The revert runner built each revert commit through a temporary index, so HEAD never moved.
6. **One re-run** of the failed job at b1e8ebfe9.
