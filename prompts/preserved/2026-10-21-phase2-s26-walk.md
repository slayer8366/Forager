# Phone walk: Phase 2 on the owner's S26, before promotion

Written by the planner (Rowan Waymark) on 2026-10-21 for the owner. RECORD -1500.
- **Build:** pre-main 02fe89f1 (1.0.3732+g02fe89f1), installed by adb. pre-main's later commits are docs only.
- **Time:** about 10 to 15 minutes, indoors or just outside. A GPS fix helps for steps 1 to 3.
- **Screenshots:** take one only if something looks wrong. They stay on your phone or laptop, never on GitHub.

Each step passes if it behaves as described. Anything else: tell me what you saw, in your own words.

| # | Do this | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maps tab, tap locate so the map follows you. Nudge the map a little with one finger and let go. | It gives a bit, about half as far as your finger, then springs back with a small bounce, and keeps following. |
| 2 | Drag the map well away, about a thumb's length. | It lets go and stops following. It stays where your finger left it. |
| 3 | With the map dragged away, switch to Journal, then back to Maps. | The map is exactly where you left it, at the same zoom. |
| 4 | Tap locate again, then pinch to zoom. | It zooms and keeps following you. |
| 5 | Turn the phone to landscape, then tap a marker near the top of the map. If none is near the top, pan one there. | Its bubble opens just under the marker, not lower down. |
| 6 | Still in landscape, run a search. | The search notice and the corner buttons sit on the same side and don't overlap. |
| 7 | Back to portrait. Open Tools from the menu, then press Back. | Tools closes. |
| 8 | Maps fullscreen, then Back. | Fullscreen ends. |
| 9 | Seasonal, then Tools > Offline maps, then Back, then Back. | First Back: Tools reopens over Seasonal. Second Back: Tools closes on Seasonal. |
| 10 | Journal: open any entry or find, then come back to the map from its bubble or "Open in Journal". | Each opens and returns as before. |
| 11 | On Maps, press Back twice quickly. | "Tap Back Button Again to Exit", then the app closes. |
| 12 | If you have time: start a short recording, then use Return to navigate home, drag the map away, and tap Return to Route. | The tilted navigation view comes back and follows you. Stop the recording afterwards. |
