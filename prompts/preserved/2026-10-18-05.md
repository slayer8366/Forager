## What the owner has ruled (quote these; don't re-derive)

- **Cloudflare, not OpenStreetMap, serves the maps** (-1281): "Cloudflare needs to be front and center for the maps serving, not OSM".
- **Street is the default** (-1282). Street uses the Cloudflare map **only if it is ready and passes its phone check before the release build**; otherwise the release ships Street on OpenStreetMap's raster tiles. **Nothing switches while the app is in use**: no runtime fallback to OpenStreetMap.
- **Where it's served:** the owner's existing mapping account, the one holding the `forager-pmtiles` Worker and the `forager-maps` R2 bucket (`server/pmtiles-worker/wrangler.toml`). Not zynergy-labs (-1272). Changes are made from the laptop (-1297).
- **Tiles:** the US archive already in R2, `us.pmtiles` (-1297, option a). The Pi planner's fresh download replaces it later, and that work isn't ours.
- **Coverage:** the whole US (-1274).
- **Design decisions,** each with Elder's phone check (`docs/audits/2026-10-18-basemap-design-review.md` on basemap-launch-plan):
  - **Paths (-1284):** dashed red-brown footpaths, dash-dot brown vehicle tracks, dotted faint informal paths, from trailhead zoom.
  - **Forest (-1285):** one solid green from the state view down (closing the z7 to z11 fade); lighter grass and scrub; parks as an outline.
  - **Labels (-1286):** a little bigger than `map-style/forager-light.json`, near-black on a light halo.
  - **Points of interest (-1287):** trailheads, parking, campgrounds, picnic sites, viewpoints, peaks, toilets, drinking water and ranger stations from mid zoom; shops and cafes only zoomed into a town.
  - **Night (-1288):** pure inversion, labels included, with the day colours chosen to survive it.
  - **Zoom (-1289):** the maximum is 18.
  - **Topo (-1291):** stays on OpenTopoMap. Topo night below zoom 9.5 crossfades over Street, so the vector Street's labels must not show through.
  - **Old offline downloads (-1294):** keep working until re-downloaded, with a one-line notice in the offline list; no forced or automatic re-download. The current `/style/offline.json` must keep serving.
