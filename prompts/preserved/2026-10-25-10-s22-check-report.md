## Method

**Install.** The phone had 1.0.3958+g1cda6c46. Its `forager.db` header read user_version 22, and the new build declares `version = 22` (`ForagerDatabase.kt:206` on `way-home-fresh-start`). So there was no downgrade. I installed with `adb install -r`, and the header read 22 afterwards.

**Fixes.** Test fixes came from the gps and network providers, one a second, with whole-second `--time` stamps and 4 m accuracy. The route was Hares Canyon Trail, using the geometry from the 2026-10-24 S22 checks:
- start 40 m to the right of the trail at c = 250;
- along the trail to c = 580;
- then a straight leg off the trail to the right, 1.25 m a second.

I could stop the walker at a chosen second. Every fix fed went to `fixes.csv`.

**Writes, observed and not assumed.** About once a second a poller copied `forager.db` and its WAL off the phone with `run-as`. It logged the new track's stored point count and the time of its last stored point whenever they changed.
- Writes landed every 30 s, between :x2.0 and :x2.9 each time: 04:07:32, 04:08:02 and on to 04:13:02, 12 writes.
- From the second write on, each held the points kept every 5 s.

**Each Return.**
- The script waited for a write.
- It held the walker on a fix the sampler would keep. That fix was predicted from the last written point plus 5 s steps, and confirmed afterwards: the next write held exactly those points (A: 04:12:03 and 04:12:08, written at 04:12:32; B: 04:12:36 to 04:12:56, written at 04:13:02). So at each Return, the newest kept point was the dot itself.
- It tapped Return 2 s later and took screenshots.
- After A, Return was tapped again to end that return, and the walker walked on.

**Measuring the gap.** The map was north-up at the same zoom for both screenshots, with the dot at pixel (719, 1953). On A's screenshot, the scale is 0.0666 m/pixel: 751 pixels from the dot to where the leg meets the trail, which is 50.0 m. The gap is measured from the dot to the nearest pixel of the way home's line: to its white casing (the low figure) and to its purple dots (the high figure).

**The line is the way home, not the track drawn as a fallback.** Zoomed out on Return B, the line's solid run leaves the trail the walker came along and runs straight off the other way. That is the trail engine's route, as on the 2026-10-24 check. The walker's own track does not go there.
