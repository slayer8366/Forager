## Amendment 2 (2026-10-24, after Sorrel's review of PR #386)

Sorrel (Fable) found M1: the gate of 8 (`TileRetryInterceptor.kt:62`) is process-wide. MapLibre runs an offline download with up to 20 requests in flight (`offline_download.cpp:417`, android-v13.5.0). The app fails the whole download on the first non-404 error (`MapLibreOfflineMapRepository.kt:367-377`, delete at `:153`).
