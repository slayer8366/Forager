## The task

Design two or three ways of showing **trail difficulty** (`sac_scale`) and **trail visibility**
(`trail_visibility`) on Street v1. Then stop, and give the owner the choice.

- **Keep Street v1 as it is** ("Street v1 + trails (Recommended)"). Change only the path styling.
- **Informal trails are already ruled:** dotted and faint (−1284), in the reserved
  `informalPath` colour. Don't reopen that. Do check how it reads beside your variants.
- **You design, you don't build** (owner: "no, for your stated reason"). Write each variant as a
  spec a coder can build into a scratch copy of `forager-street.mjs`. Give its colours, dash
  arrays, widths, and the zoom each appears from, with the day and night colours. pi-18 Part B
  builds and renders them.

## What each variant must say

- **At a glance:** what a person sees at z13, z14 and z15, in day and in night.
- **What it asks of the user** to notice or learn ("Don't make me think"). For example, whether a
  legend is needed.
- **How it holds at night** under the V1 inversion, or which named night colour it needs (−1344
  allows departures).
- **How it sits with the app's own marks:** `MapPalette.kt` and the marker swatch board. Finds
  are magenta, and tracks and the breadcrumb are drawn over paths.
- **Which `sac_scale` values it groups,** and why. The archive's real values come from pi-18
  Part A. Use those, not a list from memory.
