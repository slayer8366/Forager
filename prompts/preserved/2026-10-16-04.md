## The behaviour (the owner's step path; build exactly this)

1. **Open Forager's camera on engine A.** A row of buttons for the phone's real back lenses sits above the shutter, centred, labelled by measured zoom: S26 `.6  1  3  5`, S22 `.6  1  3  10`. The first open after the app starts is on **1**, the main wide.
2. **Tap a lens button.** The picture jumps to that real lens, and its button is highlighted.
3. **Pinch on the picture.** Zoom changes smoothly from the shortest lens's zoom up to 2x past the longest lens: S26 up to 10, S22 up to 20. The camera uses the longest real lens whose zoom is at or below the requested zoom, and crops the rest. That lens's button lights. Tapping a button still jumps straight to that lens at its own zoom.
4. **Take the photo.**
   - A JPG at an in-between zoom is saved at the usual 4000x3000, enlarged from the crop, as phone camera apps do.
   - A RAW is the chosen lens's full, untouched mosaic, plus the DNG standard's default-crop tags set from the zoom at capture. Editors that honour the tags open it cropped, and the full frame stays recoverable.
   - Everything stage 2 and -1064 / -1090 set still holds: noise reduction off on both files of a RAW shot, one exposure, the scrub, the orientation tags.
5. **Close and reopen the camera.** It opens on the lens last chosen, at **that lens's own zoom**: a pinched zoom is not remembered. This lasts while the app is open. After the app restarts it opens on **1**. This is in memory only, with no DataStore key.
6. **The row never moves; its labels turn with the phone,** like the other icons. The camera screen never rotates (-980, -981).
7. **On a phone where a lens can't give RAW, with RAW chosen,** the button stays. The photo is saved as a JPG, and the dialog's existing message line shows exactly: `This lens can't save RAW, saved as JPG.`

**With the CameraX engine** (the default, and the fallback), nothing changes: no row, no pinch. Today's camera stays exactly as it is.
