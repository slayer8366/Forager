Two photos of the tablet in landscape, described in words:
1. **Records → Offline maps:** a tall, narrow Street map, about 460 × 1000 px in the photo, beside a narrow panel (Name, Radius, tiles, Downloaded Maps, Download Maps), both centred, with wide empty margins left and right.

## The behaviour

Only on the **wide tree** (tablets and foldables, sw ≥ 600 dp). Phones are unchanged, including P11's 640 dp cap in `landscape-phone-design.md:289-298`. P11 records no reason beyond O4. The owner's words now set the tablet layout.

1. **Wherever the Journal shows a map beside its info** (Offline maps, a Journal entry's map, Records' map and Cartography's entry report, per verify-first):
   - The map is a **square (1:1)**, sized to the height available, and capped so the info beside it keeps a usable width.
   - The info sits beside it, using the rest of the width.
   - Together, the map and the info fill the content width, with no narrow centred column.
2. **The entry page** is spread out the same way: the map square on one side and the details on the other, using the width.
3. **The Journal's lists** (Entries, Records) use the width too, with no 640 dp column centred in a tablet screen.
   - Propose the form in verify-first: a wider single column with a sensible maximum, or the existing `LogPanel` wide-tree arrangement. Pick the one that reads as intentional, and say why.
4. **Portrait on a tablet:** say what happens. If the square can't sit beside the info, it stacks: the square first, with the info under it.

**Don't change:**
- tonight's rulings: the map never moves on the search reveal (-1331 to -1334); the rail order; the drawer;
- the 80% map chrome rule;
- anything on phones.
