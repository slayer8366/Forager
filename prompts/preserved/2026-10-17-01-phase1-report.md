## Item 1: hypotheses
Read from the AOSP camera service (frameworks/av; the same in android14 and android15):
- **Requests:** per-lens settings never pass through ZoomRatioMapper::updateCaptureRequest, because the zoom-updated flag is set after the logical settings (Camera3Device.cpp).
- **Results:** physical results are folded into a crop region, with the zoom ratio forced to 1.0 (Camera3OutputUtils.cpp:458-466).

| # | Hypothesis | Status |

## Mechanism prediction (Hazel)
- **Item 1:** set the per-lens CONTROL_ZOOM_RATIO to the crop, with the crop region at the full array. The fallback is the logical zoom ratio, as on the S26.
- **Item 3:** on the S26's path, the per-lens result carries the main-lens zoom folded into the crop. Report it in lens units, by dividing by the lens's zoom or reading the active physical sensor crop region.
- **Item 2:** OIS on, per lens on the S22, with the mode logged before any change. The template default is CND.
- **Item 4:** a line once a second with diopters, AF state, the near and far frame counts, and tooClose. No rule change. Candidate causes of the silent first try: AF reported "focused" while near; parked short of 0.95 x 2.5 diopters; a run broken by an unreported frame.

## Proposed Phase 2a
- crop_diag lines (requested and resulting logical and per-lens crop region, zoom ratio, active physical crop region, distortion-correction mode, OIS mode), plus a static per-lens line.
- **A stop-and-ask:** an adb-only debug extra on CameraEngineReceiver, `zoom_form crop|lens_ratio|logical_ratio`, with today's behaviour the default. Logs alone can't tell H1 from H3 or H7.
