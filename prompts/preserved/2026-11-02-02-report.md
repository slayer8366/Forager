**How the warning is decided** [read]:
- When the pin settles with signal, the app asks the Trail Map's own tile address for every zoom-11 tile of the download rectangle.
- **Each tile is read as:**
  - **no detail:** a 204, or only the land fill (`earth`), with or without the sea fill (`water` of kind `ocean`);
  - **sea:** only the sea fill; counts neither way;
  - **detail:** anything else.
- **The line shows** if any tile has no detail (A4).
- **Limits:**
  - a 750 ms wait after the pin or radius moves;
  - the latest area wins;
  - at most 4 requests at once;
  - 8 s for the whole check;
  - a 1 MiB cap per tile;
  - the first tile with no detail ends the check and cancels the rest;
  - 512 tile answers are kept for the session.
- **Re-checked** on opening, on each radius step, and when signal returns.
- **With no signal, or a failed or late answer:** no line, and one log line saying why.
- **Placement and style:**
  - portrait: directly under "Pin at";

  - `bodySmall`, default colour, padded lg/sm.

### The z11 tile cap: 36
- No latitude limit is coded anywhere. What refuses a download is the tile budget (6,000 tiles). A 24 km download stops fitting it at about 53.4° latitude. The worst 24 km cover up to there is 36 tiles. That is the cap [observed in a sweep; read in a guard test on CI].
- Every smaller area the budget allows, at any latitude, covers at most 30. So the cap never stops the check for an area that can actually be downloaded.
- `TrailMapDetailCapGuardTest` re-derives the cap, so moving the budget or the radius ceiling without it breaks the build.

### Decided beyond the dispatch (accept or reject)
1. **No wait for cached answers.** An answer the cache already holds shows at once, without the 750 ms wait. Otherwise the line would still blink on every pan inside the same tiles, which is the blink you were told the cache prevents.
2. **Where things live, differing from Phase 1b's plan:**
   - the cache is in the client, for the whole app session, not in the ViewModel helper;
   - the wait is in the ViewModel helper, not the screen;
   - the limits object is in `domain/mapdata/` rather than `data/`, because nothing under `ui/` imports from `data/`.
3. **A 200 tile with no features** counts as unread: no line, logged.
4. **Any feature in the `earth` layer** counts as land fill, whatever its kind.
5. **On a failed or no-signal answer,** an earlier answer for the same area is cleared.
6. **The cap's reading:** 36, taken from the tile budget.
7. **Test rig wiring:** the shared test rig now wires the settle callback for every test that uses it. No existing test changed count.
