1. **What a person would see**, at which zooms, day and night, and how it leaves room for the app's own marks (`MapPalette.kt`, the marker swatch board) and the trails.
2. **Prior art:** what established outdoor and topographic maps do, described generically (no product names in anything that will be committed), and what each convention protects.
3. **Does our data carry it?** Check the live US + Canada archive, `/srv/forager-build/out-usca/20261017T202051Z/forager-usca.pmtiles` (the bytes now live), by decoding real tiles: which layer, which `kind`/`kind_detail` or field, from which zoom, and how dense in a few real places (a national forest, a wilderness, a city park, a wetland region). Name the tiles you decoded. If the data isn't there, say what it would take (a tile-build change through Aspen, a new source, a sprite addition) and mark cost as inferred.
4. **Cost to the user's attention** ("Don't make me think"), and anything that could read as broken.
5. **A plain question for the owner** with two or three options and your recommendation.
