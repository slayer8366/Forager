## Why

The owner wants Puerto Rico in the map: RECORD -1274 and -1300, as cited in `build-usca.sh`'s
header. Their word of 2026-10-19 was "Publish now, add PR later". Geofabrik's `us` extract turned
out to hold neither island:

- `us-261017`'s highway cut has 0 nodes in a Puerto Rico box and 0 in a USVI box;
- `us.poly` ends at −66.88°;
- San Juan's z15 tile holds only land and ocean.

So the published US + Canada output has no data for either.

## Premises to re-check

1. That the two are missing. Re-count it yourself on `sources/osm/us-261017.osm.pbf`, a symlink
   to the SD card.
2. That Geofabrik publishes them as separate extracts, and where. Read Geofabrik's own pages; this
   is unverified. Don't assume a path from the pattern of others.
3. That `build-usca.sh` requires every region to share one replication timestamp. See its
   "extracts are from different snapshots" check under section 1.

## The questions

1. **The extracts.** Which Geofabrik extracts hold Puerto Rico and the USVI, under what region
   names and URLs, and at what size? Do they carry replication timestamps that match `us` and
   `canada` for the same day?
2. **Same snapshot.** Can a matching-timestamp set of four be had, today or on a Sunday? If not,
   what does the script's same-snapshot rule need to become? That is an owner-level question:
   describe the consequence for the map, not the code.
3. **The outlines.** Does each region have a `.poly`? Does `merge_poly.py` join four outlines the
   way it joins two? Check against `test_merge_poly.py`.
4. **Routing.** Which 5-degree squares hold the two islands? Do the current bands already include
   them (the last band runs −75° to 180°)? Would they add many nodes? Estimate from the extract
   size, marked as an estimate.
5. **What it costs.** The basemap must be rebuilt (7h44m on 2026-10-19) to carry the islands.
   Could the weekly build pick them up on its own once they are regions? What else changes:
   `USCA_REGIONS`, the coverage points (San Juan back in, and a USVI point), the README?
6. **The coverage point for the USVI.** Propose one, with its z15 tile and square worked out by
   the script's own `z15_tile` and `rd5_square`.

## Prediction and finish line

- **Prediction (the planner's, unverified):** Geofabrik publishes both as separate extracts under
  `north-america/us/`. Each is under 100 MB and shares the daily timestamp. Both islands fall in
  the last band. No new band is needed.
- **Finish line:** a report answering 1 to 6, each claim marked read (with URL or path and time),
  inferred, or could not determine. Then a short plan for the owner in behaviour terms: what the
  map gains, what the next build costs, and the one decision needed.
