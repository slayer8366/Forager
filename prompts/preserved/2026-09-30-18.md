# Dispatch 2026-09-28-310: topo-night-zoomed-out, topo night mode stays dark at every zoom

**The owner, verbatim,** with three S22 screenshots (topo night zoomed in, dark; topo night zoomed out, light; topo day zoomed out):
> "When maps are in topo night mode, they are in night mode when zoomed in, but after zooming out to a certain point, it goes back to day mode. I checked the diff between night and day and they have light colors, just a different palette"

Then, after the planner's explanation:
> "Change the zoomed out colors to match the zoomed in colors so that night mode can stay active"

**What the planner established,** headless, and to verify:
- **Night mode is not switching off.** The style has one raster layer with one fixed `NIGHT_RASTER_PAINT` (`ui/map/BasemapStyles.kt:56-62`, applied at `:86`), and nothing in `requestedMapStyle`/`mapStyleSourceFor` (`:121`, `:196-212`) depends on zoom.
- **The zoomed-out screenshot matches the V1 transform** (`nightColorOf`, `ui/map/NightColour.kt`) applied to the day screenshot's colours. These are dominant colours, quantised to 16 levels, from the owner's screenshots:

  | Day | V1 predicts | Night, zoomed out |
  |---|---|---|
  | (248,248,88) | (114,114,0) | (120,120,8) |
  | (248,184,72) | (167,103,0) | (168,104,8) |
  | (72,168,72) | (119,215,119) | (120,216,120) |
  | (56,152,56) | (135,231,135) | (136,232,120) |

- **So the cause is OpenTopoMap's own low-zoom tiles.** They are dominated by mid-dark greens (forest and hillshade), with a mean below 127.5, and V1 flips lightness, so they come out light. At trail zoom its tiles are mostly pale, and they come out dark. The screenshots are the owner's; the zoom where the tiles change is **not known**.

**The rule.** At every zoom, topo night looks like topo night does zoomed in: a dark ground, with roads and labels lighter than the ground. No zoom renders a light map. At the zooms that already look right, **nothing changes**. Day mode, Satellite (which stays day at night), and the offline vector style's night recolour are unchanged. Report on the offline style, but do not change it.
