## The behaviour (owner-confirmed step path, -1612; restate it in your report)
```
Recording, before Return
  → sundown's walk-back time uses your track (as today)
Tap Return
  → at once: your track back, as today
  → silently, the trail route is worked out (no spinner)
  → it swaps in when ready:
       ┄┄┄ your track (dotted) to where you left the trail
       ━━━ the trail (solid, same colour) to where you joined it
       ┄┄┄ your track (dotted) to your start
  → the HUD distance and sundown use the route shown, app-wide
Stray more than 50 m from the line → it re-routes once, silently
No route possible → your track, plus one note:
     "No trail routes here · following your track"
     "Trail routes not downloaded · following your track"
     "Getting trail routes… · following your track"
     "Trail routes need an app update · following your track"
     "Trail routes couldn't be read · following your track"
     "No trail on your way out · following your track"
     "Couldn't find a trail route · following your track"
     "This route leaves your downloaded area. Download a larger area to route there."
Arrive → "Arrived", with a ring at your start
```
