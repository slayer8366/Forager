## The project this starts

The owner's RAW design (RECORD -973, -974):
- **Format chip:** on the camera strip: JPG (default), HEIF, PNG, TIF, RAW+, RAW. **HDR chip:** a separate on/off chip.
- **Every format except RAW:** a RAW burst is aligned in software ("like phones do already"), averaged in floating point, HDR-merged when HDR is on, and debayered. One final photo is kept in the chosen format. RAW+ is a linear DNG.
- **RAW:** one unprocessed sensor DNG.
- **Only the final file is kept.**
- **Privacy:** make, model and time are stripped from every format (RECORD -975: "yes do that for all formats").
- **HDR exposure:** manual exposure where the phone supports it, with a fallback where it doesn't (owner: "see if we can get it to work on phones that support it, and have a fallback for those that don't").
- **Phones without RAW support:** they don't show the RAW options, and capture works as today.

**Research behind it:** `slayer8366/Forager-app` `docs/research/2026-10-13-raw-pipeline-plan-sources.md`, the 2026-09-21 field-measurement brief and its Phase 1 report, and OSCam (`slayer8366/OSCam`).

**Stages, as proposed:**
1. this probe;
2. plain RAW output with a journal preview;
3. the format chip, single frame;
4. burst plus alignment;
5. averaging and debayering (RAW+ and better JPG/HEIF/PNG/TIF);
6. HDR.

## Stage 1: what to build

A probe, behind the existing camera interface (`CameraCaptureSession.kt`), that answers once per bound camera, explicitly:

1. **RAW output:** whether `ImageCapture.getImageCaptureCapabilities(cameraInfo).getSupportedOutputFormats()` includes `OUTPUT_FORMAT_RAW` and `OUTPUT_FORMAT_RAW_JPEG`.
2. **Manual exposure, for HDR:** whether the camera reports the MANUAL_SENSOR capability (exposure time and ISO ranges), read through `Camera2CameraInfo` (`camera-camera2` is already a dependency). That class is experimental in CameraX 1.6.2: say exactly which experimental opt-ins are needed, and keep them inside the data layer.
3. **The fallback for HDR:** the exposure-compensation range and step from CameraX's standard `ExposureState`, which needs no experimental API.
4. **RAW sensor size,** active array, and the CFA arrangement, where reported, for later stages.
5. **Output encoders:** HEIF and PNG encoding availability on the device. PNG is always available through the platform; HEIF depends on the platform version and the device's encoder.

Rules:
- Every answer is **supported**, **unsupported**, or **unknown, with a reason**. Never a guessed value (CLAUDE.md, errors and failure paths).
- The result is logged once at bind and exposed as a domain model, a plain Kotlin type with no Android imports, so later stages can decide what to show.
- **No UI change.** Nothing a user sees changes in this stage.
