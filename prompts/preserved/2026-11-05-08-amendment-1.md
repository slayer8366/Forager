## A. The owner's rulings, in the order given
- **A1. The control is an icon, not a labelled button.** The owner, verbatim, to "In the Trip Planner, a \"Download Maps\" button doesn't fit beside a trip's Directions and Delete icons. Where should it go on each trip's card?": **"A maps icon next to the navigate icon."** (typed, in place of the offered options).
  - "The navigate icon" is read as the card's Directions icon, the only arrow icon on a plain trip's card.
  - The glyph is the planner's reading: the same map glyph as the bottom bar's Maps item. Its accessibility label is "Download Maps for <trip name>", which keeps the owner's words.
  - This replaces the dispatch's B1, "a labelled button, not an icon alone".
- **A2. Leaving without downloading forgets the trip** (B5). To "If someone taps a trip's Download Maps, then leaves Offline Maps without downloading, what should Offline Maps show the next time it's opened from Tools?": **"Forget the trip (Recommended)"**. The option read: "Offline Maps opens just as it does today, as if the trip's button was never tapped. No trip name is left in the box to end up on an unrelated download by mistake."
  - This is Phase 1's option B2. The page puts back what it held before the tap: pin, name and radius.
- **A3. A route too big for one download** (B3's cap). To "A route's stops can be further apart than the largest download (24 km radius). What should happen then?": **"Largest area, plus a note (Recommended)"**. The option read: "The page opens at the largest radius in the middle of the route, with one line saying the route is bigger than one download, so the user knows the far stops aren't covered."
- **A4. Cover the whole line.** To "For a route trip, should the starting area cover just the stops, or the whole drawn line between them? …": **"The whole line (Recommended)"**. The option read: "The radius starts big enough for every point of the route's line, so the trail between the stops is offline too. The stops are covered either way."
  - This replaces the dispatch's B3. The centre and radius come from every point of the route's legs (trail lines and straight legs alike), not the stops alone, and the radius is a whole number of km from 1 to 24.
  - "Covered" means inside the area that downloads, the rectangle GeoDistance.boundingBox gives. Say whether a circle test or the rectangle is used, and why. The rectangle is what actually downloads.
- **A5. The icon goes left of Directions.** To "Which side of the Directions icon should the maps icon go? …": **"Left of Directions (Recommended)"**. The option read: "Plain trip: maps, Directions, Delete. It stays one icon away from Delete, which removes the trip at once with no undo. Route trip: maps, then Delete."
  - On a route trip's card, the icon sits outside the text that opens the route, so tapping it only opens Offline Maps.
- **A6. The note's words.** To "The note on Offline Maps when a route is too big for one download. What should it say?": **"Some stops not covered"**. The words are exactly: **"Some of this route's stops are outside this area. Download the rest as a second area."**
  - The note shows when at least one stop falls outside the area at 24 km.
  - Where every stop fits but part of the line between stops doesn't, the words would be untrue. That case is a stop (C2): report whether it can happen, and don't show the note there.
  - The note shows only for a trip-opened page while the trip's starting area stands. Whether it hides once the user moves the pin or the slider comes to the planner in your report, with your recommendation.
