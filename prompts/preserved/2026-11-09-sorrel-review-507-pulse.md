- **Mechanism and cost.** One `setGeoJson` per frame on a one- or two-point source (`MapRouteStopAccent.kt:244`), for at most 500 ms per selection (fade 200 ms, pulse to 500 ms; a leaving highlight 200 ms). The loop ends when `isSettled` (`RouteStopAccentTimeline.isSettled`, both arriving and leaving checked) and `frame` stops changing, so nothing pushes at rest — confirmed from the code. While a fan is open the fan's `snapshotFlow` re-emits per frame too (`MapFan.kt:142`) and re-pushes the fan's three sources; that is what the fan already does while it animates. No layer added or restyled per frame. Battery: negligible. Robolectric cannot see smoothness; S22 only.
- **Route change mid-fade.** `plottedRouteOnCurrentStyle` no longer writes the accent's source (`PlottedRouteLayers.kt`, `filterKeys`), so a route push cannot draw the accent at rest mid-fade. The same stop moved keeps its start time (`select`, same `fanKey`); the route closed or layer off goes to none at once (`selectedStopMarker` returns null when the layer is off, `CompactMapRouteStops.kt:83/85`). Style reload: both `LaunchedEffect`s re-run and the current frame is pushed. All read, not run.

The one unverified step is Hazel's: whether `queryRenderedFeatures` reports a symbol at `icon-opacity` near 0. My reading of MapLibre's symbol query (placement/collision index, not paint) says yes, but that is memory, not source — device-only. If wrong, the gate's last resort still hides after 3 rested frames with a warning, so the worst case is the 216 ms centre-copy lag coming back for a fan opened inside the first ~100 ms of a fade, not a stuck pin.
