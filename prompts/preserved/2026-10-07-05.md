## What to find

Every place where something changes on screen with no animation:
1. **Moving between screens:** tab switches, screens opening and closing, Back, dialogs and full-screen pages.
2. **Toggles and icon presses:** the map icon bar first. Note the press feedback each control has today (ripple, highlight, its shape and clipping), and where the highlight is a hard-edged box that doesn't match the control's shape.
3. **Things that appear or disappear:** sheets, menus, chips, strip lines (sundown, back-by), banners, the navigation display, empty states, loading states.
4. **Value changes:** a number, label, icon or colour that jumps from one value to the next.
5. **Anything else that doesn't animate.**

Also list what already animates (the crossfades, `AnimatedVisibility`, `animate*AsState`, transitions), so the app's existing patterns are known.

## For each item

What the user sees now, as a tap > shows step; `file:line`; what animates nearby or elsewhere in the app that could be reused; and anything that makes it risky.
