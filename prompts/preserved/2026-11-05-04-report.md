### 2.2 When the chooser acts (the code)
1. **The waypoint's end is planned first.** If it is refused, the walker's end is never judged [read: `WayToEnds.kt:96-97`]. So the target waypoint must be one whose end settles.
2. **Good fix.**
   - The accuracy must be non-null and 15 m or less [read: `WayToEnds.kt:193`, `:251`].
   - It is the accuracy of the app's current live fix when Navigate is tapped [read: `AvailabilityViewModel.kt:407-411`, a mapped state flow that hands over its current value; `AvailabilityWaypointRoute.kt:158-161`].
   - A live fix is GPS only and within 50 m [read: `LiveFixGate.kt:78-84`].
   - The fix is judged again only on a stray of more than 50 m, or a retry after a straight-line result [read: `AvailabilityWaypointRoute.kt:160`, `:165-170`].
3. **The map must vouch.**
   - The map is read round the walker, out to the larger of the lead-in plus 160 m and 350 m [read: `WayToEnds.kt:194-196`].
   - If that read fails, if any *stored* zoom-15 tile in reach has no fence mark, or if the route ways can't be read, the walker's end goes by trail and is logged [read: `:158-163`, `:232-233`].
4. **The walker is a street end.** That means one of:
   - a mapped building within 15 m (inside one counts) [read: `StreetDropOff.kt:38-45`; inside-counts-as-zero not read in `LegGeometry`, inferred from `AccessWayMouths.kt:91-92`'s own rule];
   - built-up land use (residential, commercial, retail, industrial, school) round the walker, with a public street within 150 m [read: `StreetDropOff.kt:21`, `:46-48`].

   Anything else is a trail end: the engine's start stands and the lead-in is gated [read: `WayToEnds.kt:203`].
5. **The choice.** The first candidate whose straight leg crosses no building (the walker's own excepted), fence, wall, hedge, river, stream, ditch, canal, water area or wetland [read: `LegGates.kt:5-50`]. The candidates, in rank order [read: `StreetDropOff.kt:74-104`]:
   1. where a private way or driveway from the walker's building meets a public street;
   2. the nearest point of the street the building's address names, within 150 m;
   3. the nearest point of each public street within 150 m, shortest first.

   A "public street" is a `highway` value of residential, living_street, unclassified, service, pedestrian, road, tertiary, secondary or primary, and their links, that is not private, no or a driveway [read: `AccessWayMouths.kt:78-88`]. Car-park aisles count. Footways, paths and tracks never do.
6. **The re-run.** One re-run from the pick-up [read: `WayToEnds.kt:100-111`], then the drawn lead-in is gated again [read: `:116-123`].

### 2.4 Can it be judged from the screen alone? **No: a partial stop**
- **A pick-up chosen is silent.** It is neither logged nor marked on screen [read: `FindWayToWaypointUseCase.kt:92-95`. Only refusals (`:93`, `:155`) and by-trail ends (`:94`, `:164-166`) are logged].
- **A refusal is logged,** with every candidate's rank, leg length and gate result ("no pick-up qualified: …") [read: `WayToEnds.kt:205`, `:220-224`]. So a refusal shows the chooser ran. A success does not.
- **What the screen cannot tell apart:**
  - a pick-up chosen at the engine's own point or within metres of it;
  - a rough fix (no accuracy on the HUD, -1729);
  - a walker the map classes as a trail end.
- **What can be judged with the instruments that exist (screen plus `ForagerFix`):** a **pass** at a spot where the predicted pick-up and the predicted engine end are clearly apart, both written down before going.
- **What cannot:**
  - a "fail" (the lead-in at the engine's point), which can't be told from a wrong prediction or a classification the planner misread;
  - any judgement at an unprepared spot.
- **What a full judgement would need:** a record, at each search, of what the walker's end became (pick-up at which rank, gated engine end, by trail, refused) and why. That is new logging. **I stop here and propose no code.**

### 3.2 The rule, the screen and the log (the code)
**The off state.**
- It counts GPS fixes only [read: `WayHomeOffTrail.kt:67`].
- "Off" means further than 40 m plus the fix's accuracy from the trail route and from the walker's track from the trailhead to the start, for 15 s and at least 3 fixes [read: `:75-84`; `WayHomeWatch.kt:171`]. The walker's own track *back to* the trail is not an "on" line.
- "Back on" means within that distance for 10 s. "Back on trail" then shows for 5 s [read: `:88-94`, `:71-74`, `:31`].

**The re-route.**
- It happens on the first GPS fix, accuracy 50 m or better, with no line node within 50 m. Nodes are every 10 m [read: `WayHomeWatch.kt:154-155`, `:173`, `:183`; `WayHomeProgress.kt:86`, `:150-163`].
- It happens once per stray, and re-arms below 45 m [read: `:178-181`, `:284`]. After a re-route the new line starts at the walker, so a walker who keeps going out re-routes again every 50 m [inferred].
- The off state is kept across a re-route [read: `:228-230`]. A re-route that falls back to the track resets it and ends re-routing for the return [read: `:223`, `:229`].

**The screen.**
- **Off:** only the solid trail part dims, to 60% [read: `TrackRecordingUiState.kt:207-211`; `RouteHomeLayers.kt:296-305`]. The status line reads "Off trail · Return to Route along your track", or "Return to Route" where space is short [read: `NavigationHudText.kt:84-85`; `NavigationReadout.kt:190`, `:209`].
- **Beyond 50 m:** the route is withheld [read: `WayHomeAhead.kt:29`; `NavigationHudReadout.kt:133-137`]. The status line shows the straight distance ("… straight"), and the off words are not shown [read: `NavigationReadout.kt:186`]. The route figure refreshes every 5 s [read: `TrackRecordingViewModel.kt:857`].
- **The off-track alert also fires.** This is the "far-from-both" alert in `ReturnWatch`, which uses the same figures against the path walked out and every trail part shown [read: `ReturnWatch.kt:276-295`]. It respects silent mode and the Settings "Off-track reminder" switch [read: `:104-113`, `:291`]. On an out-and-back along one trail it should fire close to the dim's moment [inferred]. It is not the quiet state misbehaving.

**The log.** Tags `WayHomeWatch` and `FindWayHome`; all of it goes to logcat only [read: `AppContainer.kt:204`].
- "The walker is over the far band from the way home; sundown reads the track until they are back on it." [read: `WayHomeWatch.kt:166-168`]
  - This is logged on the fix that starts the re-route, unless the line below follows it.
- "The walker strayed from the way home and it was not re-routed: <why>." [read: `:190-191`]
- "The way home follows the track (<NOTE>): <reason>." This is a failed re-route [read: `FindWayHomeUseCase.kt:224`].
- "Working out the way home failed; the track is the way home." [read: `WayHomeWatch.kt:212`]
- **No line exists for** the dim, "Back on trail", a successful re-route, or the alert's delivery. Delivery logs only on failure [read: `WayHomeOffTrail.kt` has no log; `AndroidAlertDelivery.kt:120-134`].
