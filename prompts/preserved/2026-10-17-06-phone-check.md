# Phone check before the second promotion: the lint fixes (PR #246) on a real phone

Written by the planner (Rowan Waymark), 2026-10-17, for the owner's run on 2026-10-18. Forager RECORD -1197 (owed before main), -1211 (Elder's audit, item 4: write the steps down before they run).

- **Steps:** Hazel Cairnwright's, from her Phase 2 report (-1196), copied here.
- **Additions from Elder's audit:** the "watch for" lines and step 0.
- **Phone:** the **S22 Ultra** (SM-S908U), never the S26 daily driver.
- **Build:** the debug APK from pre-main's CI. Use the "app-debug-apk" artifact of the latest green pre-main run (at writing, run 39077884108 on b91de824), installed with `adb install -r`.
- **Evidence:** write each result in words. Screenshots stay off GitHub.

Each step has a pass condition. Steps 4 and 5 are observations, not gates.

## 0. Before starting (one minute)

- **Note the S22's Android version** (Settings → About phone → Software information). Elder's item B: on Android 14 and below, the new window size includes the system bars where the old figure didn't.
- **Back up Forager's data** with the app's own Backup, because step 7 can overwrite app data.

## 1. Rotation, the find page (the step that matters most)

1. Open a find with a tall photo, in portrait. **Pass:** the photo is no taller than before, with the title above it.
2. Turn the phone to landscape **without leaving the screen.** **Pass:** the photo is about 60% of the screen height, and the title and "Found at" are on screen, as on pre-main before the fixes.
3. Turn back to portrait. **Pass:** the portrait layout returns.

**Watch for:** the layout not changing until you leave and reopen the screen. The app handles rotation itself (it isn't restarted), so the new size reading has to update live. CI can't show that; only a phone can (Elder's item A). If it doesn't update, that's a **fail**.

## 2. Rotation, a day entry

In landscape, open a day entry that has a map. **Pass:** the map is about 40% of the height, the header row is above it, and the offline switch below it is fully on screen. Rotate both ways once while it's open, watching for the same failure as step 1.

## 3. Rotation, the Maps tab

Turn portrait → landscape → portrait. **Pass:** the side rail appears in landscape, the bottom bar comes back in portrait, and you never glimpse the wrong layout.

## 4. Observation, not a gate: split screen

Put the app in the top half of a split screen. Note which layout appears.

## 5. Observation, not a gate: display size

With the map open, raise the system screen zoom one step, then return it. Note whether tapping a marker still opens the right bubble and the fan-out circles are their normal size.

## 6. Phone transfer, the quick check

Run `adb shell dumpsys package com.zynergylabs.forager.app` and look at the flags. **Pass:** ALLOW_BACKUP is absent (backup still off).

## 7. Phone transfer, the real check (optional, longer)

Follow Android's documented device-to-device test with `bmgr` and the local transport (developer.android.com, "Test backup and restore", device-to-device section), taking the commands from that page on the day. **Pass:** Forager's records aren't carried: bmgr reports nothing for the package, or the restored app is empty. Restore your data from step 0's backup afterwards.

## Reporting

For each step, record:
- pass, fail or observation, in a sentence;
- the Android version from step 0;
- any step skipped, and why.

A fail on steps 1 to 3 or 6 holds the promotion until it's fixed.
