  - `adb shell am broadcast -n com.zynergylabs.forager.app/.photo.engine.CameraEngineReceiver -a com.zynergylabs.forager.app.debug.CAMERA_ENGINE --es engine platform`
    - other values: `camerax`, or `camerax-timed` (today's CameraX engine with the same measurements added)

  - Burst, with the dialog open on engine A: `… -a com.zynergylabs.forager.app.debug.CAMERA_ENGINE_BURST`
  - Summary line: `… -a com.zynergylabs.forager.app.debug.CAMERA_ENGINE_SUMMARY`
  - Read the output: `adb logcat -v raw -s CameraEngineA`
- In the app: Settings > Diagnostics > "Camera engine A (prototype)".
- CameraX is the default. A change takes effect at the next camera open. The switch is held in memory only, so a restart puts CameraX back. Remembering it across restarts is a per-case decision nobody has made, so I didn't add it.

### Is A manageable? (my assessment, from the code; nothing measured)
**Easy:** the interface we already own carried everything. The dialog, chips and every existing test needed no change; the seam is 43 lines. Matching CameraX on orientation, flash rules and image size meant reading its library and restating about 100 lines of rules, each tested against CameraX's own function. RAW and the burst were *simpler* than through CameraX: one session, no hand-over, pinning the main sensor is one setting.

**Hard:**
- The camera2 file (~900 lines of callbacks, threads and timeouts) is entirely untested and cannot be tested off a device.
- The viewfinder's surface lifetime: it must not be released while either the camera or the view still uses it. That's guarded with two flags, but untested.
- The order of open and release on pause and resume (handled with a lock).

**Expect to break on phones we can't test:**
- The flash and precapture timing (CameraX's 11 flash fixes exist for real phones).
- Session combinations the phone claims to support but then fails on.
- Odd sensor mountings or tablets in landscape (one transform branch is copied from the platform's sample and never reached on a phone).
- Phones without "is this session supported?" queries (Android 9 and older).

The fallback makes these a slower camera rather than a dead one – *if* the failure shows up as an error or a missing frame. A wrong-colour or underexposed flash shot would not trigger the fallback.

My estimate: about 2–4 weeks of device work across the two test phones to reach parity, then an open-ended tail on testers' phones.

### Decisions for the owner
- **Noise reduction on the JPG of a RAW shot:** one exposure means one request, so the JPG of a RAW shot also gets noise reduction OFF. Two requests would give the JPG its noise reduction back, but the two files would then come from different exposures.
- **Burst flash:** the burst fires no flash (the torch stays lit if it was on).
