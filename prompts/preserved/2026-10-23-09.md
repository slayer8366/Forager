- **Today's way home** is the recorded track: `routeHome()` (`domain/RouteHome.kt:140`) and `pathHome()` (`domain/PathHome.kt:98`), driven every tick by `ui/track/TrackRecordingRouteTicks.kt:83-95`, drawn by `ui/map/RouteHomeLayers.kt`. `ReturnWatch.startReturn` is at `domain/ReturnWatch.kt:185`, reached from `MainActivity.kt:667-669` → `TrackRecordingViewModel.startReturn()`.

  - `BRouterTrailRouter` runs one route at a time per process, with a 30 s limit.
  - `FindRouteDataForPointUseCase` (`domain/routedata/FindRouteDataForPointUseCase.kt:22`) covers one point, not a pair.
- **#382 M1** (Sorrel, open in -1572/-1573): cancelling doesn't stop BRouter. The fix has to land before the first production caller; suggested `invokeOnCancellation { engine.terminate() }`.
- **-1547:** with dynamic range off, an end outside the data fails plainly, but a best path that leaves the area comes back as a quiet, costlier detour. Detecting "leaves the area" is a phone-side check. Propose how.
- **The modular-code rule (-1490):** split before adding to any file over 800 lines. `ui/availability/AvailabilityScreen.kt` is 1,023 lines and `ui/track/TrackRecordingViewModel.kt` is 958. Say which files this work touches, and the split for each one over 800.
