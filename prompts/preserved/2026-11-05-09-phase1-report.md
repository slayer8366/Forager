## 2. Answers

**Q1. Shape, edges, ends**
- **The sheet:**
  - It is `ModalDrawerSheet(modifier = testTag + mapChromeContainerColor, drawerContainerColor, drawerContentColor)`, with no shape and no elevation passed (AvailabilityScreenDrawerHost.kt:105-109) [read].
  - So its shape is `DrawerDefaults.shape`, which is `ShapeKeyTokens.CornerLargeEnd`, which is `shapes.large` with topStart and bottomStart set to none (bytecode: DrawerDefaults.getShape, ShapesKt.fromToken :220-246, `end()`) [read].
  - Under ForagerTheme (Theme.kt:156) `large` is 20 dp (Shapes.kt:33). The sheet is square on its start side and 20 dp round at both end corners [read].
- **Width:** `sizeIn(240 dp, 360 dp)`, and the content is `fillMaxSize` (AvailabilitySettingsUi.kt:266, :286), so the sheet is 360 dp [read]. That leaves about 24 dp of screen beside it on the S22's 384 dp portrait [inferred, arithmetic].
- **Height:** the sheet's Surface is `fillMaxHeight` (DrawerSheet). The insets (`systemBarsForVisualComponents`, vertical + start) pad only the Column inside it [read, bytecode].
  - AvailabilityScreen sits unpadded under edge-to-edge (MainActivity.kt:409, :480) [read].
  - **So both ends of the sheet run to the window's edges.** The top corner sits under the status bar. In portrait the bottom corner sits over the app's bottom bar and under the system navigation bar [read for the layout; where the bars fall is device-only, because Robolectric insets are zero].
- **Portrait:** the drawer direction is ambient LTR (ScreenToolsDrawer.kt:174-178). The sheet sits on the screen's left. Its right edge, with rounded top-right and bottom-right corners, faces the map. Its left, top and bottom lie on the screen's edges [read].
  - That right edge also crosses what the drawer covers: the right ends of the pill and strip at the top, the bottom bar, and the Tools item's selected indicator. Tools is the last tab (AvailabilityNavigationUi.kt:109-115) [inferred; not measured].
- **Landscape, both rotations:**
  - `portEdge = mainWindowRailEdgeFor(...)`, which is always Right in landscape (AvailabilityScreen.kt:548-549, PortEdge.kt:43). So the drawer is RTL and the sheet is on the right at ROTATION_90 and ROTATION_270 alike. LandscapeRotationsAlikeTest.kt:199-212 asserts this [read].
  - Its left edge, with rounded top-left and bottom-left corners, faces the map [inferred: RTL mirroring of the end corners; the test will confirm].
  - Its right side lies over the rail on the screen's edge. There is no bottom bar in landscape.
