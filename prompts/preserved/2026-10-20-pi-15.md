# Dispatch 2026-10-20-pi-15: measure the islands' routing squares before the rebuild

From Aspen Meridian, the Pi planner, to a Pi coder. Written 2026-10-20 at `pi-build` `68a3c328`.
**A measurement only.** It downloads two small extracts into a scratch directory and builds routing
from them there. Nothing is installed, committed, published or written outside the scratch
directory.

The owner's word, verbatim, through the question tool: "Measure it first (Recommended)". The option
read: "A coder downloads just the two island files (about 77 MB, into a scratch folder) and builds
their routing alone — minutes, not hours — to get the real square sizes. Then we know before
committing 12 hours."

## Why

The coverage check needs a square over 100,000 bytes at each point. pi-14 estimated the USVI's
square, `W65_N15`, at about 250 kB from Hawaii's ratio, and called the estimate shaky. A square
under the floor would refuse the four-region rebuild at validation, about 12 hours in. Puerto Rico's
square, `W70_N15`, is measured too.

A second question rides along. pi-14 inferred that the islands' 261017 files carry the same header
timestamp as `us-261017` and `canada-261017`, which is `2026-10-17T20:20:51Z`. If they do, the
rebuild can use `--local 261017` and reuse the extracts already on the card, not download 18.8 GB.

## Do

1. **Scratch directory.** Make `/tmp/pi15-islands/`, owned by forager-build or by you. Everything
   goes there, and it is removed at the end, apart from the report.
2. **Download** the two dated files and their `.md5`s:
   - `https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/us/puerto-rico-261017.osm.pbf`
   - `https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/us/us-virgin-islands-261017.osm.pbf`

   Use the build's user agent, `HTTP_USER_AGENT` from `pins.env`. Check both MD5s. Their published
   sizes are 74,075,487 and 3,122,047 bytes (pi-03).
3. **Header check.** Run `osmium fileinfo -g header.option.osmosis_replication_timestamp` and
   `…_sequence_number` on each file. Compare with `us-261017` and `canada-261017` through
   `/srv/forager-build/sources/osm/`, read-only.
4. **Routing, the same way the build does it** (`build-usca.sh` section 6):
   - `osmium tags-filter` each island file with `USCA_ROUTING_FILTER`;
   - `osmium merge` the two cuts;
   - BRouter's three steps (`OsmFastCutter`, `PosUnifier`, `WayLinker`), with the same profiles and
     arguments, under a modest heap, in the scratch directory.
5. **Measure.**
   - List every `.rd5` produced, with its size.
   - Run the build's own coverage arithmetic: `rd5_square` and `z15_tile`, loaded from
     `/opt/forager-build/map-build/bin/build-usca.sh` the way the tests load them, for `san-juan`
     and `charlotte-amalie` from the installed-or-repo `pins.env`. Say which you used.
   - Give each point's square, its size, and whether it passes `> 100000`.
6. **Is this a fair stand-in?** In the real build, the last band, −75:180, also contains any other
   data that falls in those two squares. With only `us`, `canada` and the islands as inputs, little
   else should, though the islands' outlines overlap. Count the nodes the published `canada-261017`
   and `us-261017` cuts hold inside each square's box, with a small `osmium extract -b` into
   scratch. If either holds any, the real square would be larger, never smaller. Say so either way.

## Prediction and finish line

- **Prediction (the planner's):**
  - both headers read `2026-10-17T20:20:51Z`;
  - `W70_N15` is well over 1 MB;
  - `W65_N15` is between 100 kB and 1 MB, so it passes.
- **Finish line:** a report covering:
  - both MD5 results;
  - the header timestamps and sequences next to `us`/`canada`'s;
  - each square's size, and pass or fail;
  - the stand-in answer from step 6.

  Each claim is marked measured or read, with the command and time. The scratch directory is
  removed, and that is confirmed.

## Privacy

The report gives square names, sizes and counts. It gives no coordinates beyond the two pinned
coverage points, which are already in the repo. No IP addresses or network names.

## Stops

Stop and report, never choose:

- an MD5 mismatch;
- any write outside `/tmp/pi15-islands/`;
- touching `/opt` contents, `/etc`, units, the timer, `/srv/forager-build` data or the SD card's
  files (reading through `sources/osm` is fine);
- a refusal by the permission system. Do not route around it.
