## Build
1. **A new table, `cartography_entry_track_paths(entryId, trackId, path)`.**
   - Primary key (entryId, trackId), with an index on trackId, and no @ForeignKey (the ForagerDatabase rule).
   - `path` is the track's read-seam-filtered lat/lng in timestamp order, encoded compactly: for example two doubles per point, or an encoded polyline.
   - The codec is pure Kotlin, with round-trip tests, including an empty path and one point.
2. **MIGRATION_16_17** creates it. That means `version = 17`, `SCHEMA_VERSION`, the exported `17.json`, and SchemaMigrationTest's steps and chain.

   - `openForRestore` must migrate a v16 backup to 17.
3. **Copy at delete time.** `DeleteTrackUseCase` copies the path into a row for **every** ref row naming the track, whether kept or withheld, and whether the entry is a draft or not, **before** deleting.
   - The copy and the delete run in one Room transaction if feasible. Otherwise the copy is idempotent and runs first.
   - Say which, with file:line.
   - The track's own points are still deleted, as the delete-data page says.
4. **Deleting an entry deletes its path rows,** alongside its refs.
