## 1. Placement: "A, plus 25 m" (Blocking)

**What is built** (`domain/wayhome/WayHomeProgress.kt:114-131`): the place is the earliest node, at or after the previous place, within 25 m of the fix (`:120`); failing that the earliest at or after the previous place within 50 m (`:121`); failing that the earliest anywhere within 25 m, then within 50 m (`:122-123`); else off the line. The remaining figure is the line from that node, plus the hop when its band counts it, plus 25 m on a trail part (`:128-131`). The line is filled to 10 m nodes (`:28-57`), so a walker on the line is always within 5 m of a node.

**Where it holds.** For a fix on or near the line, moving homeward, the earliest node within 25 m at or after the previous place is at most ~5 m ahead of the walker's true place, so the figure is never short by more than 5 m and the 25 m allowance covers that. The first placement (no previous) is the earliest node anywhere, never short. The switchback and loop tests (`WayHomeProgressTest.kt:38-45`, `64-77`) pin this, each with `previous = null`.

**Where it is short: the "not before the last place" rule (`:120-121`), combined with a later leg of the line within 50 m.** Two cases, both on the PR's own hairpin fixture (`WayHomeLines.kt:20-24`: legs 20 m apart), worked by hand from the code, not run (no local Gradle; CI only). Node indices below follow `WayHomeLine`'s fill: trackBack nodes 0-5 (north −50..0), leg 1 nodes 6-25 (east 10..200, north 0), the 20 m riser nodes 26-27, leg 2 nodes 28-47 (east 190..0, north 20), trackToStart 48-57; `alongMeters[i]` = 10·i.

- **Case 1, one noisy fix, no turning back.** The walker is on leg 1 at east 90, placed there (previous index 15). One GPS fix inside the gate (`LIVE_FIX_MAX_ACCURACY_METERS` is 50 m; the gate's own doc records outdoor GPS fixes up to 20 m reported and worse under canopy) lands at (100, 30): 30 m from leg 1, 10 m from leg 2. `:120` finds no leg-1 node within 25 m at or after index 15, so it takes the earliest leg-2 node within 25 m: (110, 20), index 36, 22.4 m away, band NONE. Remaining = 570 − 360 + 0 + 25 = **235 m**. The truth from leg 1 at east 100 is 100 + 20 + 200 + 100 = **420 m**. Short by 185 m. Worse, the place is now index 36 on leg 2, and `:120-121` never looks before it again: the next, accurate fix at (110, 0) finds (110, 20) at 20 m and stays on leg 2. The figure stays short by about 200 m until the walker reaches the hairpin's corner at (200, 0), where nothing at or after index 36 is within 50 m and `:122` recovers.
- **Case 2, a double-back through the hairpin.** The walker has come round onto leg 2, placed at index 32 (east 150), then turns back: east along leg 2, round the riser, west along leg 1 (going away from home). At (200, 10) nothing at or after index 32 is within 50 m, so `:122` places them at the riser, index 26; from there, every fix west along leg 1 finds a leg-2 node at or after index 26 within 50 m (`:121`: (190, 20) is 44.7 m from (150, 0)), and the place walks west along leg 2 while the walker walks west along leg 1. At (100, 0): index 33, hop 44.7 counted, remaining = 240 + 44.7 + 25 = **310 m** against a true **420 m**. At (0, 0): 140 + 44.7 + 25 = **210 m** against **520 m**. It recovers only at (0, −40), when (0, 20) is more than 50 m away.

Both cases need two legs of the line within 50 m of each other, which is what a switchback is, and the first needs one fix in error by more than 25 m towards the other leg, which the gate admits. The "most recent stored point" rule the owner ruled on (`PathHome.kt:15-24`) is never short in either case: "after a double-back it overstates by at most the length of the leg just walked twice". -1613 ruled "A, plus 25 m... so never short"; the report's claim for A was "never short by more than 25 m" (report §2, option A). The implementation does not meet that claim. **Blocking, by the brief's rule and the owner's ruling.**

**The one-line test that shows it** (for the planner; not written by me): on the hairpin, `previous = WayHomePlace(index of (90, 0), 0.0, NONE, true, 0.0)`, fix `at(100.0, 30.0)`, assert `remainingMeters >= 420.0`. By the arithmetic above it gives 235. (Revert check b1's own failure messages, §11, confirm the arithmetic's frame: nearest-point on the same fixture at (100, 12) gives 225, which is 210 + 15; my 235 is 210 + 25 with the hop in band NONE.)

**Options, for the owner, not chosen here** (the "not before" rule exists so the needle and the drawn line do not jump back to leg 1 while walking leg 2, which is a navigation convenience; the figure is the safety input):
(a) Take the *figure* from the earliest node anywhere within reach (`:122-123`'s search, ignoring `previous`), and keep the "not before" place for the needle and the line ahead only. Never short by construction; the needle may still point the wrong way on a parallel leg, as today's design accepts. `82da028e2` already separated the needle's aim point from the place (§9), so this split has a home.
(b) Search one band at a time across the whole line before narrowing: earliest within 25 m at or after previous *or* within 50 m at or after previous, whichever is earlier. Fixes case 1 (leg 1 at 30 m is earlier than leg 2 at 10 m); does not fix case 2.
(c) Drop "not before" entirely: always earliest anywhere. Never short; the needle regresses to leg 1 whenever leg 2 passes within 25 m of it, which the rule was added to avoid.
Any of these changes a safety input and is the owner's call (CLAUDE.md, "stop-and-ask").

A BRouter `OutOfMemoryError` (one route is about 25 MB over the app, `BRouterTrailRouter.kt:48-49`; RECORD -1615 has three OOM map tests this week) escapes the job: `job` is never set to `null` (`:178`), so every later stray logs "already being worked out" and nothing re-routes for the rest of the return, and `_shown` keeps a `null` result (track shown, which is safe). ## 4. The stray judge

`WayHomeWatch.kt:122-158`. Gate: GPS only (`mayAct`, `:126`), accuracy ≤ 50 m or null (`:127`), only while returning and only with a trail line shown (`:123-126`). Hysteresis: < 45 m re-arms (`:139-142`), 45-50 m nothing (`:145`), > 50 m judged (`:146-158`). Confirmed against the ruling's figures (constants at `:229-234`, tied to the hop band's 50/45).

**Can it loop?** Not unboundedly; the three stops hold as written (`:147-149`, `:157`, `:180`). What the actual rate is, said plainly, because the report (§4, "Once per stray") implied fewer re-routes than this gives:
- **L-395-3** After a re-route publishes, `progress = null` (`:183`) but `armed` is left as the last fix set it. The new line's `trackBack` starts at the most recent stored point, which lags the walker by 27-40 m (`RouteHome.kt:97-100`), so the walker's next fix is usually within 45 m of the *new* line and re-arms at once (`:139`). A walker who keeps heading away therefore gets one re-route per ~50 m of further straying, not one per return. At walking pace that is one engine run per 30-40 s at most, each bounded by the engine's 30 s limit and the one-route lock, so it cannot run away; but "once per stray" in practice means "once per 50 m", and the owner should hear it that way. Not wrong against -1613's words; a figure worth recording.
- A stray judged during the first-of-return job is logged and starts nothing (`:148`); after publish, `armed` is still `true` from `find` (`:95-101`, `:201-204`), so the next fix over 50 m re-routes. Correct.
- When a re-route falls back to the track, `_shown.line` is `null`, so `onFix` returns at `:125` and the judge never runs again this return; `reroutingEnded` (`:180`) is a belt to that brace. The "fell back" reason at `:147` is therefore unreachable (**L-395-7**); harmless.
- **L-395-4** `WayHomeProgress.kt:114`, `WayHomeWatch.kt:129`: after an `OffLine` result `previous` is `null`, so the next `placeOnWayHome` starts with `previousHopBand = NONE`. The far band's hysteresis (enter above 50, leave below 45, `PathHome.kt:170-173`) is lost across off-line: a walker at 48 m is placed (COUNTED), at 51 m off-line, at 49 m placed again. `WayHomeAhead.kt:17-18` says the route is "withheld as the far hop withholds it today"; today's withhold has the hysteresis. Pass the last band through `OffLine`, or keep `previous` across off-line for the band only.
