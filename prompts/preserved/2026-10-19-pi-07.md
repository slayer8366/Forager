## The mechanism, as observed in pi-04 (re-check it yourself first)

On `systemctl stop` of a transient unit with default `KillMode=control-group`, three things
happen:

1. `on_exit` runs, but `$?` is the last finished command's status, usually 0. So
   `if [[ $code -ne 0 ]]` skips everything.
2. A process forked within about 1 ms of the stop is killed too.
3. The first `log` in `on_exit` writes to the killed `tee` and bash dies of SIGPIPE, before
   `keep_failed_run` and `cleanup_scratch`.
