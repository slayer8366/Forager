## 2. The tile budget (-1679's parked question)

### What happens today on a v3 download (read)

- A region's tile count is MapLibre's `completedTileCount`, taken at the download's end (`MapLibreOfflineMapRepository.kt:204`) and on every listing (`:247` → `A/map/OfflineRegionReconciliation.kt:102`).
- MapLibre counts every stored tile of every source, a "no content" tile included (Sorrel 416 §7: `offline_database.cpp:1058-1068`, `:678-683`).
- The Worker answers a missing reference tile 204 (`index.ts:603`), and MapLibre treats 204 as "no content" (Sorrel 416 §6, `http_file_source.cpp:172-177`).
- The reference source spans z10-15 with no `bounds` (Sorrel 416 §3), the same as the download's range (`A/domain/OfflineMapRepository.kt:159-160`). So a v3 region stores one band tile for each basemap tile. That doubling is inferred from those reads; it has not been observed on a phone.

**The four readers of that count** (the dispatch named two; four exist):

| # | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `A/ui/availability/OfflineRegionsList.kt:124-127` | "Tile budget: x / 6000", fed `tilesUsed = sumOf { tileCount }` from `AvailabilityOfflineMapsUi.kt:288` |
| 2 | `AvailabilityOfflineMapsUi.kt:225-236` | the picker's "~N tiles — exceeds your remaining budget of R" |
| 3 | `A/ui/availability/AvailabilityViewModelOfflineMaps.kt:116-126` | the gate in front of a new download |
| 4 | `AvailabilityViewModelOfflineMaps.kt:41-46` | the same gate on "Download again" for a restored row (not named in the dispatch) |

The per-region figure is also shown as "Tiles" in the region's details (`A/ui/log/RecordDetailsContent.kt:218`).

**The figures.**
- A 24 km region is 4,405 basemap tiles at 45.357°N, and 5,246 in the worst case (`OfflineMapRepository.kt:216`).
- On v3 it would show about 8,810 to 10,492.
- After one such download the line reads over 6,000. Readers 2 to 4 then see a negative remaining budget, and every further download is refused, a 1 km one included. The estimate side stays the basemap's, as -1678 ruled.

**A fifth thing the user sees, also doubled: the progress line.**
- While downloading, "x / y tiles" (`A/ui/availability/OfflineMapsSideBySide.kt:164`) shows MapLibre's resource counts (`MapLibreOfflineMapRepository.kt:402-403`).
- On v3 a user who was shown "~4,405 tiles" would watch a count to roughly 9,000 (inferred from the tile doubling).
- This is not one of the dispatch's two figures, but it is the same mismatch, and it makes the user think.

**MapLibre offers no per-source count.** `javap` of the pinned `android-sdk-13.5.0-runtime.jar` (local Gradle cache) shows `OfflineRegionStatus` with:
- `getDownloadState`, `getCompletedResourceCount`, `getCompletedResourceSize`;
- `getCompletedTileCount`, `getCompletedTileSize`;
