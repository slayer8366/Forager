## The planner's diagnosis: confirm it, or show where it's wrong

The planner reproduced this on production (`b8ac1e2c`) in Chromium at 390x844 with touch. The script and a screenshot are at `~/Zynergy/device-evidence/2026-10-12-panel-overlap/planner-repro/overlap.py`, and you run it as `overlap.py BASE_URL W H OUT.png` with the venv below. The steps: choose the pilot layer, tap a drawn cell, open the panel, then measure every visible child of `#panel`.
- **Result:** `#readout` has a box of 35 px and content of 311 px. Every other child fits.
- **The mechanism the planner infers:**
  - In the phone layout, `#panel` is a flex column (`index.html:57`). When opened it is a fixed-height box that scrolls (`#panel.open { top: 15% }` at `:65`, with `bottom: 0`), and its children have the default `flex-shrink: 1`.
  - Flex children normally can't shrink below their content, because their automatic minimum height is their content. `#readout` has an explicit `min-height: 2.6em` (`index.html:33`), which replaces that automatic minimum.
  - So when the opened panel's content is taller than the panel, `#readout` is the one child that shrinks, and its text overflows visibly over its siblings.
- **Why the existing checks passed:** `phone_check.py` checks that the readout is visible and inside the panel, but never whether its content fits its box, and never in the opened panel with a long readout. It's a check that never saw the case that could fail it.

Confirm this mechanism, or show where it's wrong, **before** writing the fix.

## The fix (your choice of method; say why)

- **Rule:** in the phone layout, no child of the panel is ever smaller than its content, in either state, on either layer, with or without a readout. The panel scrolls instead.
