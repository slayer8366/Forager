### Decisions you need to look at
1. **Close-focus rule** (you asked for it to be reported as my call):
   - **Telephoto to main:** the telephoto sits at 95% or more of its minimum-focus diopters, with AF not focused, for 15 frames in a row (about 0.5 s). Then the main lens takes over, cropped to the zoom, and its button lights.
   - **Back to the telephoto:** the main lens reads focused, farther than the telephoto's limit with a 0.8 margin (50 cm for the 3x lens), for 15 frames.
   - **Moving onto a telephoto:** if the main lens's last focused reading is already too close, the main lens is kept from the start.
   - **No focus distance reported:** the rule never fires, and that is logged.
2. **Highlight style.** The dispatch listed this as a stop example. I made the call from the overlay rule ("fill colour carries meaning"): the lit lens's number is yellow, the camera's existing meaning colour; the others are white, and all are outlined. Please confirm with the owner.
3. **JPG sizes don't match RECORD -1107 on the S26.** On the S26 the main lens and the 5x list no 4000x3000 JPEG in the dumpsys tables; their largest 4:3 size is 4080x3060. I used "largest 4:3 size" as each lens's own size:
   - S26: 4080x3060 on the ultra-wide, main and 5x; 3648x2736 on the 3x.
   - S22: 4000x3000 on the ultra-wide and main; 3648x2736 on the 3x and 10x.

   This also means 1x JPGs on the S26 change from 4000x3000 to 4080x3060.
4. **Lens choices that change what engine A does at 1x and on failure:**
   - All three streams are physical on the chosen lens, even at 1x.
   - If a lens's session won't configure, the camera falls back to CameraX, as engine A already does; it does not try another lens.
   - A lens whose RAW stream won't configure is treated as a lens without RAW and gets the no-RAW line.
5. **How the crop is applied:**
   - The S22 uses each lens's own crop region.
   - The S26 uses the camera's overall zoom setting, because it lists no per-lens crop.
   - The viewfinder only makes up the part of the crop the phone reports it didn't do; if the phone reports nothing, the viewfinder adds no zoom.
6. **Lens memory and smaller behaviours:**
   - A lens reached by pinching is remembered, as well as a tapped one. The main lens standing in for close focus is never remembered.
   - The shutter waits up to engine A's existing 3 s for a lens switch to finish. While it waits it looks disabled, which is the dialog's existing behaviour.
7. **Main-wide rule only partly shared.** The lens set uses the shared rule and the new shared view helper (`LensView`, which also now backs `ReachRoles`). Camera2's older main-wide restatement is still there, but it only runs when there is no lens set.
