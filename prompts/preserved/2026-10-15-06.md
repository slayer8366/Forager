## What to build

A probe the planner can trigger over USB, with the camera dialog open. Use a debug-only broadcast or intent extra received by the debug build. Name it and give the exact `adb` command.

**The runs.** The probe performs four runs of 8 back-to-back captures each:
1. RAW+JPEG, AE and AWB unlocked;
2. RAW+JPEG, AE and AWB locked;
3. RAW-only, unlocked;
4. RAW-only, locked.

**Locking.** "Locked" means setting AE and AWB lock and waiting until the capture results report `LOCKED` before the first frame. It goes through the experimental interop, confined to the debug source set, as stage 2 confined it to `CameraXRawCapture.kt`. The lock is released in a `finally` on every path.

**Logged once per frame,** under one tag, as one machine-readable line per frame (JSON is fine):
- submit time, and arrival time of each image;
- sensor timestamp;
- exposure time, ISO, and post-RAW sensitivity boost;
- AE, AWB and AF states, and focus distance;
- dynamic black level and white level;
- the time to copy the RAW plane;
- a sharpness figure: the mean absolute green gradient on a downsampled green plane.

**Logged once per run:**
- `maxMemory` and the in-use heap before and after;
- `getOutputMinFrameDuration` and the stall duration for RAW_SENSOR at the bound size, from the stream configuration map;
- the bound RAW and JPEG sizes.

**Frames are discarded.** Nothing is written to storage, the journal or the Gallery. The camera returns to the user's chosen format afterwards, with a rebind if needed. The probe must never leave the camera dead or in a different format.

**Failure is reported explicitly.** A run that cannot bind, a lock that never reaches `LOCKED` within a timeout (propose one), or a frame that never arrives is logged as such, with its reason. Nothing is guessed or padded.
