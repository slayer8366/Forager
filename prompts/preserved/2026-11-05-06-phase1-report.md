# Dispatch 2026-11-05-06, Phase 1 report: the shaded band at the edges of see-through map surfaces

Hazel Cairnwright, 2026-11-05. Base: pre-main `ee0651fe0`, checked against the remote after a fetch. The dispatch was read at records-after-173 `55aa3f994`. Nothing has been fixed, no PR opened, and nothing pushed to pre-main. RECORD.md and the audit index are untouched, and the phone was not used.

Tags: **[read]** means code or text I read, with file and line. **[observed]** means pixels I measured. **[inferred]** means reasoning I did not measure.

## Summary

- **The band is the platform's elevation shadow, seen through the 80% fill.**
- **The renderer draws that shadow as a ring with a hole.** It thinks the surface is opaque, so it skips the part it expects the surface to hide.
- **The hole has hard, square corners.** Its distance from the edge is set by the corner radius, not by the elevation.
- **Why the renderer thinks the surface is opaque:** Compose passes the platform the layer's opacity (1), not the fill's (0.8).
- **Two shadows make two edges.** There is a strong spot shadow, pushed away from the light at the top of the screen. There is also a faint ambient shadow at exactly the corner radius in.
- **The owner's crop matches this to about 1 dp.** Both edges are visible in it.

## 1. The cause, shown from pixels

### The owner's crop [observed]
`bubble-edge.png`, 416×806 px, read pixel by pixel.

- **Middle.** Over plain grey map the middle reads (231,227,216). That is exactly the 0.8 composite of SurfaceContainerLight #F4EFE2 (`Color.kt:135`) over the bare map beside the bubble, (181,180,176): (231.4, 227.2, 216.0). So **the middle has no shadow at all.**
- **Band.** The band reads (223,219,208). Solved per channel, that is the map darkened by **0.23 ± 0.015**, the same in R, G and B, so the shadow is black.
- **Spot edge.** The inner edge is a hard step taking about 2 px. On the right it is at 71 px in, at the top 92 px in, at the bottom 53 px in. The inner rectangle sits about 20 px low.
- **Ambient edge.** A second step, of 1 level, sits at 75 px in on the right and 76 px in at the top (x 296 to 299, y 200 to 215).
- **The shadow continues outside.** Directly below the bubble the map is darkened (153,148,144), fading over about 50 px.
- **Scale.** The bubble's corner fits a 73 px radius. Against `RoundedCornerShape(20.dp)` (`MapBubble.kt:181`), that is about 3.65 px per dp.

### The code path [read]
1. `MapBubble.kt:175-186`: a Surface filled with `MapIconStackButtonColor*`, which is 0.8 alpha (`MapChrome.kt:54, 66, 69`), with `shadowElevation = 6.dp` and `tonalElevation = 3.dp`.
2. Compose 1.12.0 (bytecode, `GraphicsLayer.configureOutlineAndClip`) calls `Outline.setAlpha(getAlpha())`. That is the layer's alpha, which is 1, not the fill's.
3. Android's renderer works out `casterAlpha = node alpha × outline alpha` (`libs/hwui/pipeline/skia/ReorderBarrierDrawables.cpp:142-143`, AOSP main). It passes `kTransparentOccluder_ShadowFlag` only when that is below 1 (`:214-217`).
4. Skia's GPU fast path (`src/gpu/ganesh/SurfaceDrawContext.cpp:1121-1325`, Skia main):
   - Without that flag, it draws the spot shadow as a ring inset `blur + max(blur, offset)` from its outer border (`:1253-1296`).
   - With the flag, it widens the ring into a full fill (`:1256-1258`; the ambient shadow at `:1202-1205`).
5. `ShadowRRectOp.cpp`:
   - It sets `umbraInset = max(outerRadius, blurRadius)` (`:263`).
   - It builds a 9-patch whose inner edges are straight lines (`:456-474`, `:551`).
   - So the hole is a **square-cornered rectangle** about `max(corner radius, blur)` in from the shadow's edge.
6. Android's defaults (`core/res/res/values/dimens.xml:753-757`): light_y 0dp, light_z 500dp, light_radius 800dp, ambient alpha 0.039, spot alpha 0.19. Together, 1 − 0.81 × 0.961 = **0.222**, against the crop's 0.23 ± 0.015.

### Does it reproduce in Robolectric? Partly. Robolectric's rendering differs from the phone's.
Robolectric's real shell, day, over #B3B3B0 [observed]:
- the band is 8/255 below the composite;
- it ends in a hard step (19/255 summed RGB) at 8.0 dp in at the sides, 14.7 dp at the top and 3.3 dp at the bottom;
- night behaves the same.

Differences from the phone:
- **The band is narrower,** 8 dp at the sides against the phone's about 19.5 dp.
- **It doesn't follow the corner radius.** With 4 dp corners the Robolectric band is the same 8 / 14.7 / 3.3 dp [observed]. On the phone the radius sets it [read].
- **The inner corners are rounded,** not square [observed].
- **There is a faint even darkening inside,** 2/255 (centre E5E1D7 against composite E7E3D8). The crop does not show this [observed].

Why [read]: Robolectric takes Skia's tessellated path (`SkShadowUtils.cpp:608` onwards; `SkShadowTessellator.cpp:300-345`), not the GPU ring. That Robolectric renders on the CPU is [inferred]. So a Robolectric test can show that **a shadow is drawn under the fill**. It cannot show the phone's band width or shape, and that needs the phone.

Shapes whose corners differ from one another skip the fast path on the phone too (`SurfaceDrawContext.cpp:1142-1145`) [read]. On the phone those probably look more like Robolectric's render [inferred]. That applies to `MapCardCorners.BOTTOM`, `TOP`, `besideBar` and `outerSide`.

**A. No shadow on see-through map surfaces.**
- Looks like: the fill reads evenly at 0.8 right to the edge, with no frame and no dark halo outside (variant 04 [observed]).
- The edge then shows only by the fill and, where there is one, the hairline. The tile, type panel, legend, icon bar, cluster, control pill, pill, strip and card have a hairline.
- The bubble, the dialogs, the OK/Cancel row, the menus and the snackbar have no hairline. Over a map of similar colour they may stand out less. J8 already listed "whether they still read as separate from the map without a shadow" as a device item.

**B. Shadow outside the shape only.**
- **B1:** the platform's own shadow with its inside clipped away (variant 08). Inside it reads the exact composite. Outside it matches today's pixels [observed]. It looks like the shadow an opaque card casts, heavier below.
- **B2:** a drawn blur outside the shape only (variant 09). It looks similar, but hand-tuned, and it does not follow the light.
- For menus and the snackbar this may not be possible: Snackbar has no elevation parameter, and the menu's popup bounds may clip an outside shadow. Could not determine.

**C. An outline instead.**
- The map-chrome hairline (`MapChromeOutline.kt:34`, 1 dp, Bark 40% or white 40%) with no shadow (variant 10: the exact composite from 1 dp in [observed]).
- Looks like: a crisp thin line, as on the icon bar, bottom bar and top header, which the owner asked for (RECORD -966/-967).
- On surfaces that already have a hairline, C is the same as A.

**D. Tell the platform the surface is see-through, so the shadow is filled.**
- A layer alpha just under 1 (variant 07). There is no hard edge and a soft lighter rim, but the whole card reads evenly 8/255 darker than the 0.8 composite [observed].
- This relies on the renderer's `casterAlpha < 1` test and forces an offscreen layer.
- The fill no longer composites to 0.8.

**E. Compose's own drop shadow behind the fill.**
- Variant 11: like D, evenly 7/255 darker with a soft edge [observed].
- It does not depend on the platform. Clipped to outside the shape, it becomes B2.

**Not fixes, for information:**
- **F. A lower elevation** does not remove the band on the phone. The hole's distance is `max(corner radius, blur)`, and the shadow's strength does not depend on elevation, so a 2 dp pill still has bands about 16 dp wide [inferred].
- **G. Opaque fills** go against the owner's 80% rule.
