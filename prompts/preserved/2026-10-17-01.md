## Change

Four things on engine A, on the dev build only:

1. **The S22's zoom crop.**
   - **What happens:** on the S22 the zoom between lenses never reaches the saved JPG (-1148). With the main lens standing in at 9.99x, and with lens 6 at a 1.10x crop, the log read `crop_achieved=1.00`. The viewfinder stretched the preview instead, and the JPG came out uncropped.
   - **What works:** the S26, which takes the overall-zoom path because it lists no per-lens crop, cropped correctly every time (-1147).
   - **Ask:** find out why the S22 doesn't apply it, with evidence, then fix it so the JPG and the viewfinder show the same zoom.
2. **Stabilization.** Engine A requests no stabilization: a `git grep -i stabilization` over `photo/engine/` on dev d94e16f4 found nothing. The S22's lenses 5, 6 and 7 offer optical stabilization (`availableOpticalStabilization [0 1]` in the saved tables). Request optical stabilization on the lens in use wherever it's offered, and log the mode the result reports.
3. **Log units.** On the telephotos, `viewfinder_crop` and `jpg_shot` report `crop_achieved` in main-lens units (4.90 on lens 7 with no crop asked, -1147). Report it in the chosen lens's own units, matching `crop_requested`.
4. **The silent close-focus try.** On the S26's first close-focus try nothing fired, and no readings were logged (-1147). The second try worked. Add a rate-limited line with the focus distance, AF state and the rule's frame count while on a telephoto, so a silent case can be seen next time. Change no rule.
