## The behaviour, as the owner confirmed it

**T3, the line**
- Start recording > the map's top strip shows **"Sunset 6:42 PM · start back by 5:32"** when the walk-back time is known, or **"Sunset 6:42 PM · in 1 h 12 min · dark 7:13"** when it isn't yet ("dark" is civil dusk).
- After 5:32 passes > **"Sunset 6:42 PM · start back was 5:32"**.
- Tap Return or Navigate > the same line moves into the navigation display (HUD).
- After sunset > **"Sun set 6:42 PM · dark in 21 min"**.
- After dark > **"Dark since 7:13 PM"**.
- No position yet > **"Sunset: finding your position…"**.
- Recording started after dark > **"Dark since 7:13 PM"**, with no countdown to tomorrow.
- **The start-back time is always the one the leave-by alert uses.** It never says there is enough time. That sunset assumes a clear, flat horizon is stated once, in Help, not on the line.

**T4, Settings**
- Settings > **"Sundown"** section > **"Sundown alerts"** switch, on by default. Off stops the notifications only; the line stays.
- **"Dark under trees"**: 30 min / 45 min / **1 h** (selected) / 1 h 30, with the line **"Woods get dark before sunset. Alerts allow this much extra."**
- Changing it > the start-back time on the line moves at once. Both settings survive closing the app.
