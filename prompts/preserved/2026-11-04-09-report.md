### How it works
- **Where the copy lives:** `files/offline_basemap/<area id>/`, as files, not Room. Each tile is gzipped and written whole or not at all, beside an empty marker for each 204 and one "whole" record per area.
- **What is fetched:** only the area's zoom-15 tiles that MapLibre's store doesn't hold with the fence mark and the copy doesn't hold yet.
- **The limits:**
  - 4 requests in flight, 10 started a second;
  - it stops at a connection failure or 3 errors in a row;
  - it refuses an area over 4,000 tiles or a tile over 512 KiB.
- **When it runs:** opening Offline maps with signal, signal returning, "Try again", and a download completing. With no signal, no pass runs and one line is logged.
- **The read order:** MapLibre's marked tile first, then the copy's marked tile, then exactly today's read. Where neither store holds the mark, the end still goes by trail with its log line (A10).
- **The shown size** includes the copy (A5). The tile budget doesn't count it (A6).
- **The mark has one definition:** `NearbyMapData.kt:128` now calls `BarrierMark.carriedBy` (P7). The reader's existing tests are unchanged and green, at pre-main's counts: `NearbyMapDataTest` 8 of 8, `FindWayToWaypointDropOffTest` 10 of 10.
- **The log line that makes a server regression visible** (RECORD -2113) is `DropOffMapFetch.kt:109`. Every pass that leaves tiles wanted logs it, with "N without the mark" counted.
