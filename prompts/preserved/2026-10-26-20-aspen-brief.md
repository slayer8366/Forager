## 2. A trail-names file per area (step 7 PR 4, the wrong-turn alert; RECORD -1631)
The owner: "A names file from the Pi (Recommended)", per area, from the same extract as the routes, stored beside them; the wrong-turn alert fires on named trails or roads only.
- **Why the Pi:** the routing data carries no names (BRouter's lookups.dat has no `name` or `ref` key; the app keeps only points and length, routing/BRouterTrailRouter.kt:104-118), and the alerts run in the background service where map tiles aren't readable (step 7 report docs/audits/2026-10-25-04-off-trail-and-wrong-turn-report.md §2.1, §4 Q1(a), on off-trail-report).

Format is Aspen's to propose; Forager will own the reader. Cut per area by the Worker beside the routes (server/pmtiles-worker/src/routing/, src/rd5/).
- **To report:** the proposed format, a size per area (the report guessed a few hundred KB, unmeasured), and how it is cut.
- The spike's finding stands: compare names, not OSM ways (one street is many ways).

## 3. The elevation build (routing first; terrain shares the DEM)
The owner's "Everything" (-1628) includes elevation in routing; the hilly walk (-1687) gave a climbing allowance the app now builds, which for trail routes waits on this.
- **Routing:** BRouter reads elevation only from its own `.bef` files, built from 1° `.hgt` (docs/navigation/2026-10-21-elevation-sizing.md on records-after-173: USGS 3DEP 1″, public domain, ~130 GB; NRCan MRDEM as Canada's fallback; build 3″ first, keep raw-to-hgt at 1″; GDAL not yet on the Pi). Aspen's own topo rulings take 3DEP 1″ and MRDEM for Canada (Forager-cartography, ruling 5) and one DEM build shared with terrain (ruling 6).
- **App-side changes this will need (the planner's, not Aspen's):** the profile's `consider_elevation = false` (app/src/main/assets/routing/profiles2/hiking-mountain.brf:13) flipped; and the app's adapter currently drops the engine's per-point elevation (BRouterTrailRouter.kt:114; TrailRoute has no height field, domain/routing/TrailRouter.kt:41-45). So routed squares with elevation won't change anything visible until those land.
- **First numbers to report:** the measured `.bef` store at 3″ (the sizing's 2-6 GB is unmeasured), build time, and whether the weekly unit grows.
