**Q1. How Tools > "Offline Maps" opens the page.**
- **The row:** `OfflineMapsEntryRow` (`AvailabilitySettingsUi.kt:166-181`), under the Trip Planner and Map Layers folds (:288-299) [read].
- **The tap:** `buildOpenOfflineMapsFromTools` (`AvailabilityScreenJournalReturn.kt:22-45`) [read]. It:
  - records where the user came from (`ToolsOfflineMapsReturn`: tab, Journal top tab, Records chip, fullscreen), unless an origin is already stored;
  - closes the drawer and ends fullscreen;
  - sets `PendingJournalDestination.VIEW_OFFLINE_MAPS` and switches the tab to `CompactTab.JOURNAL`.
- **Where it lands:** JournalTab picks Records and asks for the Offline maps chip (`JournalTab.kt:429-433`). RecordsTab selects the chip (`RecordsTab.kt:235-240`), fires `onOfflineMapsOpened` once (:247-251), and composes `OfflineMapsPanel` (:394-409) [read].
- **There is no route.** The app has no navigation library, only enum state (`RecordsTab.kt:62-64`) [read]. The page shows in **Journal > Records > Offline maps chip**.
- **Back:**
  - First Back puts the original page back, with its tab, chip and fullscreen, and reopens Tools (`AvailabilityScreenBackHandlers.kt:75-87`).
  - Second Back is the drawer's own handler, which closes it (:62-64).
  - The Journal's own Back is off while this is pending (`AvailabilityCompactScaffold.kt:1368`).
  - Once the user moves to another chip or tab, the origin is forgotten (`ScreenToolsOfflineMapsReturn.kt:94-103`). Back is then the Journal's own, then the tab handler to Maps (`AvailabilityScreenBackHandlers.kt:88-93`) [read].

**Q2. How the pin is chosen when the page opens.**

**Q4. Routes, the slider and tiles.**
- **What a route trip stores:**
  - `PlannedTrip(id, name, location, date?, route?)`, where the location is the first stop (`PlannedTrip.kt:23-35`).
  - `PlottedRoute(stops, legs)`: ordered stops; leg *i* joins stop *i* to *i+1*; at least 2 stops; no two consecutive stops at the same position; A → B → A allowed (`PlottedRoute.kt:9-30`).
  - A stop is a copy of a waypoint (name, position; :36-37). A leg is either a trail line (`Trail`) or a straight line (`Straight`) (:39-82) [read].
- **The slider:** 1 to 24 km in 1 km steps (`AvailabilityOfflineMapsUi.kt:210-218`; `Region.kt:10`; `OfflineMapRepository.kt:248`; clamp at :251) [read].
- **Radius to tiles:**
  - The area is a rectangle reaching the radius in each direction (`GeoDistance.boundingBox`, `GeoDistance.kt:69-83`). The download uses the same rectangle (`RegionRouteData.kt:177`, `MapLibreOfflineMapRepository.kt:330-337`).
  - Tiles are counted at zooms 10 to 15 (`EstimateOfflineTileCount.kt:44-69`; `OfflineMapRepository.kt:170-171`, :206) [read].
  - 24 km costs up to 5,246 of the 6,000-tile budget (`OfflineMapRepository.kt:227`) [read].
- **The budget check:** 6,000 tiles (:146) minus every downloaded area's estimate (`OfflineTileBudget.kt:20-23`). It shows live under the slider (`AvailabilityOfflineMapsUi.kt:226-237`) and is enforced on Download (`AvailabilityViewModelOfflineMaps.kt:131-142`, message at `AvailabilitySearchStates.kt:92-98`) [read].
- **Can a route need more than 24 km?** Yes, by construction.
  - Nothing limits how far apart stops are (`PlottedRoute.kt:18-30`, `RoutePlotDraft.kt:14-15`; a grep of `ui/route`, `domain/route` and `domain/routedata` found no limit).
  - A leg between stops with no downloaded area is a straight line (`PlotRouteUseCase.kt:76-82`) [read].
  - So two waypoints more than about 48 km apart make such a route. Whether any route on the owner's phone does is [not verified].

**B. B5: what a trip's pin, name and radius do after the user leaves without downloading.** Today the user's own point (after a Cancel), name and radius stay (Q3).
1. **Treat them like the user's own entry.** They stay until a download succeeds or another trip replaces them. Nothing extra to build.
   - Risk: a later Tools > Offline Maps opens on the trip's pin with the trip's name in the box. A user who pans elsewhere and downloads saves the area under the trip's name unless they notice. That is a silent wrong label.
2. **Forget the trip on leaving.** Leaving without downloading puts back what the page held before the tap: pin, name and radius.
   - Tools > Offline Maps then always opens as it does today.
   - Cost: about 70 lines in a new file, plus tests.
- **I'd recommend option 2.** It never shows the user a trip they didn't ask for there, and avoids the mislabelled area.
- Either way, today's quirk with a cancelled entry is unchanged. That is a separate item for the planner.
