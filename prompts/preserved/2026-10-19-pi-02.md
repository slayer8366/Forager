## Phase 1: prep, read-only

For each item, report:

- **Size counting hard links,** and what deleting it would actually free (`stat -c %h`, `du -sh`,
  `find -links +1`).
- **Whether anything reads it:**
  - running processes (`lsof` or `/proc/*/fd`);
  - the published output's `manifest.json`, which names its inputs and outputs by path;
  - `build-usca.sh`, `build-orwa.sh` and the systemd units (`git grep` the path).
- **Whether it holds anything not kept elsewhere.** Item 5's log has a copy in `logs/`, as read by the planner. Its
  `metrics.jsonl` may not: check, and if it is the only copy, propose keeping the small files and
  deleting only the large ones.
- **A recommendation for each item:** delete, keep, or keep part, with the reason.

Then stop, and ask the owner per item.
