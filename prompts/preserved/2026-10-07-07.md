## Why

The owner, verbatim: "Next sweep is data friendliness: raw data is useful and honest, but put plainly in a UI amounts to clutter to a user, no matter how useful it is. Adding visuals like pie charts, graphs, in a tabled format (as opposed to line by line), with clear and intuitive labels. Intuitive design creates less need to think." And: "My idea is to strike a fine balance between speed/utility and visual polish".
