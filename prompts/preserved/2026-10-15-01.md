## Where this sits

- **Format chip:** JPG (default), HEIF, PNG, TIF, RAW+, RAW. **HDR chip:** separate.
- **RAW** is one unprocessed sensor DNG: no averaging, HDR or debayering.
- **Only the final file is kept.**
- **Make, model and time are stripped from every format** ("yes do that for all formats").

Stage 2 decisions:
- **-1002:** "Show it now. Nobody will see it before release but me". The chip appears in this stage with JPG and RAW.
- **-1003:** "2 A". A DNG that can't be cleaned is discarded, the shot's cleaned JPG is kept as the photo, and the user sees: "The RAW file couldn't be cleaned of camera details, so this photo was saved as JPG."

Stage 1 readings (-996, -998): both test phones support RAW and RAW+JPEG through CameraX. The S22 Ultra's RAW is 4000×3000 GRBG; the S26 Ultra's is 4080×3060 GBRG.

Facts reported by the erased stage 2 coder, read from CameraX 1.6.2 and AOSP. **Re-verify each; none is checked by this planner:**
- CameraX writes no GPS into a DNG. DngCreator writes Make, Model, Software (the full build fingerprint), DateTime, DateTimeOriginal and UniqueCameraModel, in IFD 0.
- UniqueCameraModel is required by the DNG specification.
- The platform has no DNG metadata writer (ExifInterface writes JPEG, PNG and WebP only).
- With RAW+JPEG output the JPEG comes out at the RAW size, and `onImageSaved` fires once for both files.
- Backup copies only files named in `log_photos`.
- The app has no photo share; export (Gallery or folder) is the path.

## What the user sees (step path)

1. **Camera screen:** on a phone where stage 1's probe reports RAW for the bound camera, a chip on the strip reads **JPG**. Elsewhere there is no chip and capture is as today.
2. **Tap the chip:** a menu shows **JPG** (ticked) and **RAW**, each with its approximate size (-975).
3. **Pick RAW and take a photo:** the photo saves. Its JPG shows at once as the placeholder, then as the full-size, zoomable preview and the thumbnail (-1004). The chip stays on RAW, including after the app restarts (-1005).
4. **If the RAW file can't be cleaned:** the shot is kept as JPG and the -1003 message shows.
5. **If storage is low:** the shot is taken as JPG with a plain message, never a failed shot.
6. **Export:** sends the RAW file. The JPG is never a separate photo (-1004).
7. **Delete:** removes everything the shot saved.

## Stage 2: what to build

1. **The chip:** JPG (default, today's behaviour, output byte-for-byte unchanged) and RAW, shown only where the probe reports RAW for the bound camera. Thin-line style and outline as the strip's other controls (-970). The choice persists across restarts in DataStore, like the grid mode, with JPG as the first-run default (-1005).
2. **RAW capture:** a RAW+JPEG capture (`OUTPUT_FORMAT_RAW_JPEG`). **A new path**, not a conditional threaded through the JPEG path (CLAUDE.md, Building). The JPEG is scrubbed exactly as camera JPEGs are today.
3. **DNG cleaning:** a small TIFF-tag editor of our own, in the data layer:
   - removes or blanks Make, Model, Software, DateTime and DateTimeOriginal;
   - replaces UniqueCameraModel with the neutral value "Forager", if the specification requires the tag;
   - removes any GPS IFD;
   - leaves colour matrices, black and white levels, CFA, active area and orientation untouched;
   - on any failure, follows -1003 (fail closed) and logs the cause.
4. **Storage:** the DNG beside its JPEG under `photos/`. The photo row records the DNG; if that needs a Room column, it is version 22 with `MIGRATION_21_22`, and the column lands with its reader (CLAUDE.md). Check that 22 is still unclaimed on every remote branch before taking it. Backup and restore carry the DNG; delete removes both files.
5. **Operating limit:** RAW at the standard 12 MP size only (-655, -975). Propose a free-storage margin below which the shot falls back to JPG.

   - It never writes Make, Model, Software, DateTime, DateTimeOriginal or any GPS.
