## The work

1. **Flush panel in landscape.** The search dropdown's top equals the search bar's visible bottom edge (its divider) in landscape, with no gap, and portrait stays as it is. Measure before and after.
2. **Keyboard keep-in-view in landscape, including a floating keyboard.** Whenever the IME overlaps the dropdown's bottom row (docked or floating; read real IME insets or bounds, not only a viewport shrink), scroll the panel so Search and Set on map sit just above the keyboard's top. When it floats, the panel stays on its own side ("keep it to the one side"): don't move or resize it sideways. A user drag stops it, as in portrait (#202). Robolectric can't make a floating IME, so test with injected IME insets and say what stays device-only.
3. **Clear resets the search to default.** Clear clears the region and results (as now), plus the species or category selection back to the default (the default category, as on a fresh start) and the bar's summary to its default text. Recent searches stay. This replaces the coder's earlier "Clear keeps the species" call (RECORD -728).
4. **The bar never drops out of line with the strip in landscape**, whatever its text. If the summary is longer than the bar's room, it ends in "…" and the join holds. No layout fallback may put the bar below the strip. Test with a long species name in landscape at 780 and 823, both rotations, after a rotation from portrait.
5. **A search moves the map.** Any search (recent, coordinates, Set on map, Search with current location) stops the camera following the GPS fix and moves it to frame the searched area (SightingsMap.kt:880-899 is where following blocks it today). The locate button resumes following. Test through the real screen and ViewModel that a recent search far away puts the map camera at that region.
