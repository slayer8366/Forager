## Build (the owner's seven points)
1. **A stack** is markers whose touch areas overlap at the current zoom. The same markers are no longer a stack once the zoom separates them.
2. **Tapping a stack** fans its markers out in a small ring around their spot. Each keeps its own icon, with a thin line back to its true position. The map does not move.
3. **More than 8** fan out in a spiral, so none overlap.
4. **Tapping a fanned-out marker** opens its bubble, as a tap opens it today.
5. **Folding back:** tapping the map, panning, zooming or pressing Back folds them back. Back closes the fan-out before anything else it would close.
6. **The fan-out takes 0.4 s.** With the system animator scale at 0, the markers appear spread at once. Fold-back uses the same timing.
7. **Everywhere:** the Maps tab and a journal entry's map, on the phone and the tablet, from the shared map code rather than per-screen copies.
