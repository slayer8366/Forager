## What to build

A second dev-only probe, triggered over adb with the camera dialog open, like the piece 1 probe:

1. **Borrow the camera.** Take it from CameraX the way the investigation's option (b) describes: CameraX releases it, then the platform camera interface opens it.
   - Log the time from trigger to the camera opening on the platform side, and from there to the first RAW frame. This is the swap pause.
   - Log any viewfinder freeze or blink the user would see, as far as the app can tell.
2. **A platform capture session.** It has a preview surface (the probe's own, or a dummy, whichever the stream combination needs) and a RAW_SENSOR reader at the 12 MP size, with enough buffers for 8 frames.
3. **The bursts.** Four runs of 8 back-to-back RAW frames:
   1. default processing, AE and AWB locked after convergence;
   2. the same with noise reduction OFF and hot-pixel correction OFF on the burst requests, where the phone offers those modes;
   3. a manual-exposure run at the converged exposure and ISO, if MANUAL_SENSOR is available;
   4. one run with lens-shading mode OFF, if offered. Record whether the RAW changes.

   Use a repeating request or captureBurst, whichever delivers the sensor rate. Record which one was used and why.
4. **Logged per frame,** one JSON line under its own tag:
   - sensor timestamp and arrival time;
   - exposure, ISO, frame duration;
   - every processing mode actually reported in the capture result: noise reduction, hot pixel, edge, shading, tonemap if present, black level lock;
   - dynamic black and white levels;
   - lens-shading map mode;
   - the copy time.
5. **Logged per run:**
   - frames per second and the spread between frames;
   - the time from the first to the eighth frame;
   - heap use;
   - the reported minimum frame duration and stall.
6. **Saved:** each run's 8 frames as single-frame DNGs, through Forager's own writer (`ForagerDngWriter`, with the same forbidden-strings check as stage 2). The planner can then measure noise on tripod pairs offline: default against NR off.
7. **Hand-back.** Close the platform session and let CameraX resume the dialog's binding. Log the time until the viewfinder is live again and the dialog's format chip is unchanged. Every path, including a failure mid-run, must hand back and release locks in a `finally`. The camera must never be left dead.
8. **Explicit failure.** A mode the phone doesn't offer, a stream combination that won't configure, or a frame that never arrives is logged as such with its reason. Nothing is guessed (CLAUDE.md).
