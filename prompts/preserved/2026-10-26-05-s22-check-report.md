## Method

**Install.** The phone had 1.0.3987+g8895f80f, the #404 diagnostic build. Its `forager.db` header read user_version 22. The new build declares `version = 22` (`ForagerDatabase.kt:206` at `b0014e7e`). So there was no downgrade and no version downgrade (3994 over 3987). I installed with `adb install -r`, and the header read 22 afterwards.

**Fixes.** Test fixes from the gps and network providers, one a second, whole-second times, 4 m accuracy (the previous Hazel's feeder, copied and re-pointed). The walk, at 1.4 m/s:
- start S (the 2026-10-25 walk's start), 30 m due east to K, 280 m due south to E;
- Return tapped at E, then 70 m due west to W, held there;
- Return tapped again to end the return, the map reset to north and re-centred, then 50 m due north from W (the V run).

**Recording.** Started with the map's Start recording button, which always records in High accuracy (`MainActivity.kt:647`). Kept points came every 5 s (62 on the saved track, timestamps 5 s apart while moving).

**Writes, observed and not assumed.** About once a second a poller copied `forager.db` and its WAL off the phone and logged the new track's stored count and last stored point. Writes landed every 30 s, at :18 and :48 past each minute, 13 of them while points were being kept. The W run spans the writes at 07:08:18, 07:08:48 and 07:09:18; R spans 07:11:48 and 07:12:18; V spans 07:18:48 and 07:19:18.

**Measuring.** The map is north-up and follows the dot, so the dot sits at a fixed pixel (719, 1593 on W and V). For each screenshot:
- the dot's centre is the middle of its blue fill on the dot's own column (fill radius 24.5 px, white ring to about 40 px);
- the gap is the distance from that centre to the nearest pixel of the recording line's purple (`MapPalette.breadcrumb` 0x650BB1). The way home's azure (0x0288D1) and the dot's own blue do not pass the purple test;
- the scale is 0.0572 m per pixel, fitted on V from the west leg's row against the fed fix's distance north of W: 26 shots, residual 0.64 m rms, 1.22 m at most. On W the east leg gives 0.0555 to 0.061.

**What the line would have been without the change.** For each shot, the distance from the fed fix to the last point the store held at that moment (from the poller). That is where a line drawn from the store alone ends, before even the screen's 15 s poll.

## Step 1: walking

**W (37 shots, 07:08:08 to 07:09:38).** On the dot's column the lowest purple pixel is 41.5 to 42.5 px from the centre in all 37 shots: the purple runs to the white ring and under it.
- At those times the store alone would have ended up to 39.3 m back, 10 m or more in 28 of the 37.
- **The W shots sampled only two phases of the 5 s cadence:** 19 shots 0 s after a kept point and 18 shots 3 s after. A gap at 4 s was never sampled. That is why I added V.

**V (43 shots at 1 s, 07:18:46 to 07:19:29; 36 measured).** The 7 not measured are the first 7, while the new leg was still too short to run along the dot's column.
- Every phase was sampled, 6 shots each at 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 s after a kept point, plus 7 with the walker standing.
- At 0 and 1 s the purple reaches the ring (42 px). At 4 s a gap shows: 68 to 82 px, 3.9 to 4.7 m, when the fix was 5.6 m past the newest kept point. At the next kept point it closes to 42 px again, every time (5 cycles).
- So the measurement does see a gap when there is one. The gap is what the 5 s cadence leaves, and nothing more.
- The store alone would have ended up to 40.3 m back, 10 m or more in 22 of the 36. The points kept from 07:18:53 to 07:19:13 were drawn before they were written at 07:19:18.
