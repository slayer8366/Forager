## Verdict

**Not mergeable as it stands: one High finding, with a small fix.** The design is right and most of it is verified
from MapLibre's own source: the `night/` names cannot be overwritten by the late sprite load, the renderer takes
them from the first update, the 61 night sheets equal the owner's rules pixel for pixel (my own decoder, not
Hazel's), the day sheets are R2's to the byte (four-way agreement), and the five revert checks are real. But one of
the six switched references, `places_locality`'s `icon-image`, is an expression MapLibre Native 13.5.0 **refuses at
parse time** (H1): its day value is a `step` on `["zoom"]`, and the switch wraps that in a `match`, which puts
`["zoom"]` below the top level, a form the parser rejects with `"zoom" expression may only be used as input to a
top-level "step" or "interpolate" expression`. The Android SDK reports that refusal only in MapLibre's own log, so
the app counts the reference as switched and the new log line reads `6 image references switched` either way (M1).
On the phone: place dots and capitals stay in day colours at night below zoom 8, on v2 and v1 alike, and the
dispatch's pass condition ("61 added and 6 switched") passes anyway. The fix is to wrap the `step`'s outputs
rather than the `step`, plus a unit test holding the parser's rule; after that, and with the device checklist
widened (M2), this is mergeable into pre-main with the S22 night check as the gate before promotion.

## What was checked, in the order asked

### 1. The ordering claim

**Verified from source at `android-v13.5.0`** (fetched, not quoted from Hazel's report; line numbers are from the
fetched files):

- **A sprite load replaces same-named images and nothing else.** `Style::Impl::onSpriteLoaded`
  (`style_impl.cpp:369-397`) walks the loaded images; for each whose id is already present it does
  `*found = std::move(*it)`, and the rest are appended and sorted. An image named `night/parking` is never the
  sprite's `parking`, so it is neither replaced nor removed. Hazel's `:374-381` is the replacement loop; correct.
- **The renderer gets the night images from the next update.** `Style::Impl::addImage` ends in
  `observer->onUpdate()` (`:317`), and `Map::Impl::onUpdate` (`map_impl.cpp:107-131`) snapshots
  `.images = style->impl->getImageImpls()` into the update parameters. So the images added in the callback are in
  the renderer's view before any frame drawn after the callback.
- **A tile does not wait for the sprite if its images are already there.** `ImageManager::getImages`
  (`image_manager.cpp:121-147`): before the sprite is loaded, a requestor whose dependencies are all present is
  notified at once; otherwise it waits for the sprite. A tile whose icons all resolve to `night/…` names lays out
  without waiting; one that needs a day name (H1, or a name with no night copy) waits for the sprite, as it does
  today.
- **The sprite arrives after the callback.** Not re-observed by me; taken from Hazel's S22 lines in the verify
  report (`nonNull=0/61` at +0 ms, `61/61` at +234 ms and +222 ms on two launches). The design does not depend on
  the timing either way.

**Inferred, not verified:** that `Style.addImages(HashMap)` on the Android SDK hands each bitmap over with the
pixel ratio from its density (Hazel's `javap` check of `Style.toImage`; the SDK sources jar is not in the Gradle
cache here). The night copies are given `DENSITY_DEFAULT * pixelRatio` (`NightImageApply.kt:52-54`), and both
indexes carry `pixelRatio` 1 and 2 respectively (checked). The `@2x`-above-1 choice (`:29`) matches MapLibre's
sprite URL choice; the app sets no pixel ratio of its own (no `pixelRatio` call in `app/src/main`).

**Where the first-frame claim fails:** H1. One reference is refused, so its layer draws day images from the
first frame and keeps them.

### 2. The fallback, and the `match` wrapper against the real expressions

  input not among them takes the `otherwise`, the day expression itself (`:99-105`). Both are in the log line
  (`:142-146`), and `NightImageSwitchTest:159-173` asserts the drawn image and the line's tail. Verified. The
  style's `metadata["forager:sprite-icons"]` is also diffed against the night names (`:112-113`), so a future v6
  name the app lacks is named even when no literal uses it.
- **The six references, read from the served v2** (`server/pmtiles-worker/src/street-style-v2.json`, byte-equal
  to `map-style/forager-street-v2.json`; v1 likewise): `landuse_forest_glyphs`/`fill-pattern` `"tree_dark_irregular"`,
  `landuse_wetland_tufts`/`fill-pattern` `"tufts"`, `roads_oneway`/`icon-image` `"arrow"`, `roads_shields`
  (a `match` on `network` with three `concat` outputs), `pois` (a `match` on `kind` with a `["get","kind"]`
  otherwise), `places_locality` (a `step` on zoom). v1 has four (no patterns), with a different `pois` match.
- **Every original branch survives in the emitted JSON:** the day expression `E` is embedded verbatim as the
  input, inside the `concat`, and as the `otherwise`; `NightImageSwitchTest:46-54` asserts exactly that shape and
  `:137-145` runs it against the three expressions for `peak` and for `""`. For `roads_shields` and `pois`,
