## The style

The spec is `prompts/preserved/2026-10-20-trail-design-handover-rowan.md` on pi-build. The work is in
`map-style/overrides/forager-street.mjs`, regenerated by `generate-street.mjs` to its three copies.

**The layers.** Street v1's `roads_path_foot` becomes 17 layers, so the style has 90 layers against 74. Bottom to top:

- `informal_z13`, `informal`
- `steps`
- `unknown_z13`, `hard_z13`
- `unknown_z14_{clear,faint}`, `hard_z14_{clear,faint}`
- `{unknown,easy,rough,hard}_{clear,faint}` from z15

The values are exactly the spec's. Night: `informalPathNight #bb8a6f` for both informal layers. Hard keeps the
inversion, `#ffad9f`.

**The rest of the style is untouched.** I checked this by parsing both versions against the `5b270ac4` style:

- the only layer removed is `roads_path_foot`;
- 17 layers are added;
- 0 other layers changed;
- the order of the existing layers is the same;
- the only metadata changes are `forager:palette` (`hardPath`, `informalPath`, `informalPathNight`),
  `forager:night-layers` (the two informal entries) and the new `forager:night-bands`.

**Cross-check against the Pi reference.** I applied the Pi coder's r2 diff to `5b270ac4` in scratch. The patched file's
sha256 is `c283d4a7…b076`, the one the handover names. I then generated from it:

- Layer by layer, against this build, only two filters differ: `unknown_z14_{clear,faint}` list the same three
  clauses in a different order.
- `check-street-trails.mjs` passes on the Pi's style too. All other fields are equal.

**Engine.** In the pinned style-spec 26.4.4, `line-dasharray`'s data-driven support lists js 5.8.0, and android and
ios point to maplibre-native #744.

- The 13.5.0 `libmaplibre.so` (arm64, from the cached AAR) has a cross-faded data-driven binder only for `Image`.
  That fits "unsupported", but it is an **inference, not proof**.
- Either way the layers don't depend on it: every layer uses a constant dasharray.

## The night band

- `NIGHT_LAYER_BANDS` in `forager-street.mjs` gives the two informal layers `{ forest: { min: 1.9, max: 2.3,
  belowNightOf: "footpath" } }`.
- `check-street-night.mjs` applies the band only to that layer on that ground, and also requires the ratio to be below
  the night footpath on the night forest (computed, 2.43).
- Every other layer and ground pair keeps `NIGHT_FLOOR = 2.5`, which was not edited. That includes informal on night
  grass (4.09) and night ground (5.33).
- A band naming a layer with no night exception, or an unknown ground, is a problem.
- Day pairs were added: informal (3.10 on ground, 2.45 on forest) and hard (day and inversion).
