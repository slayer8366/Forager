The day patterns come from the style's own sprite, not from the app's runtime `addImages`
(`NightImageApply.kt`). So the missing pattern is **not specific to the night images, or to runtime-added
images.** Two things are observed. Patterns are missing at whatever zoom the camera holds when a style load
happens (the cap, and about z15 in step 5). They stay missing at that zoom for new ground too (step 3), and
on return to it (step 4). Changing to a different zoom shows them.

That is closest to the report's **S**, widened: a style load at a given zoom, not the night images. It weighs
against **R**: R is about runtime images being removed, and the sprite's day images are not added that way.
It does not separate S from **D**. This is inference from screenshots: no log shows which images a tile
holds. The report's Part B logging build would be the next evidence, and it was not run (Rowan: "Skip the
report's Part B").
