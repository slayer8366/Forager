## Verdict

**Mergeable into `pre-main`; no blocking finding; four should-fix, of which one is a step-path question for
the owner and one is about the CI harness rather than this PR; five nits.** The rule is one headless object
(`FindLinkedPhotos.kt:22-33`): a photo is hidden while a find that is saved and located links to it, by the
Room cross-reference and never by position, and it is applied at both places that build photo markers
(`GetMapRecordsUseCase.kt:104-106`, `GetCartographyEntryMapDataUseCase.kt:108-110`). I traced every other
path that could draw a photo and found none that bypasses those two (section 1). All four rulings hold at
both call sites, each with a test over a real database, and the fail-first run and two revert runs I read
fail with messages only their own edit could produce, on build logs with no Kotlin compile errors (section 5).

The should-fix findings: on the Maps tab, a deleted find's photos come back only on the next tab switch,
not when the Undo window ends, and a find deleted from its page returns the user to the Maps tab during that
window, so the common path shows the find vanish and the photos stay gone until Maps is left and re-entered
(S1; disclosed in the PR body; owner's step path to confirm). The Maps tab's read-failure fallback hides
every linked photo, including a draft's and an unlocated find's, and the only log line says the finds could
not be loaded (S2). The `loadMapRecords` race is real, pre-existing and not made likelier by this PR, but
S1's natural fix adds an entrant to it, so the two go together (S3). The revert runs' `unit-test-report`
artifacts carry stale XML from a different run, with failures those reverts could not cause (S4; the job logs
are clean, and the coder's figures come from the logs).

### Should-fix

**S1. Ruling 4 on the Maps tab: the photos come back on the next tab switch, not when the Undo window ends.**
The map's records are a snapshot taken when the tab comes into view
(`AvailabilityScreen.kt:518-521`, `LaunchedEffect(isMapsTabShown)`; `AvailabilityMapDisplay.kt:52-53`).
During the Undo window the find is still in Room, so a reload hides its photos
(`GetMapRecordsUseCase.kt:104-106`) and the pending slot hides the find (`DrawnMapRecords.kt:22`). When the
window ends, `commitFindDelete` deletes the row and calls `onFindDeleted` (`MushroomLogPendingDeletes.kt:145-148`),
which removes the find from the snapshot and nothing else (`AvailabilityMapDisplay.kt:71-75`): the photos it
hid are not in the snapshot and stay absent until the next `onMapShown`. A find opened from the map and deleted
from its page returns the user to the Maps tab at once (`AvailabilityScreenJournalReturn.kt:104-108`), so the
ordinary path is: on Maps, the snackbar counts down, the find vanishes, its photos do not appear. The PR body
says so ("They appear the next time the Maps tab is shown"), and the test models a reload
(`FindPhotosOffMapTest.kt:255-256`), not the stay-on-tab path. The ruling's words, "after a find is deleted and
its Undo window ends, its photos are standalone again", read as the window's end, and "don't make me think"
counts a find that vanishes with nothing in its place. Two fixes: `onFindDeleted` re-reads
(`scope().launch { loadMapRecords() }`, and its comment "there is nothing to wait for" is no longer true: the
photos are), which needs S3 first; or the owner accepts "next time the map is shown" and the S22 step says
where the user stands when the window ends. Either way the S22 step must say it, or the runner files either
result.

**S2. The Maps tab's read-failure fallback fires without a line that names it.** When the finds cannot be
read, `hidingFindIds` is `null` and every photo linked to any entry is left out
(`GetMapRecordsUseCase.kt:104`, `FindLinkedPhotos.kt:32-33`), including a draft's and an unlocated find's,
which rulings 2 and 3 say draw. The comment at `GetMapRecordsUseCase.kt:102-103` says the failure "is already
in failures, which the caller logs"; the caller's line is "Couldn't load finds for the map."
(`AvailabilityMapDisplay.kt:105-107`) and says nothing about photos. The entry map's line does
(`GetCartographyEntryMapDataUseCase.kt:141`). CLAUDE.md, Errors: a default fallback is logged when it fires.
Remedy: the FINDS case of that log line names the photos left out, one clause. The choice itself (hide rather
than draw) is defensible, since the alternative draws photos apart from finds that are not drawn either, and
the failure state already shows no finds at all; but it is a decision the dispatch did not make, and under it
two rulings do not hold. One line to the owner, for Rowan to judge; I would not hold the merge on it.

**S3. The `loadMapRecords` race is real, pre-existing, not made likelier here, and S1's fix meets it.**
Every `onMapShown` launches a fresh read without cancelling the last (`AvailabilityMapDisplay.kt:52-53`,
`:103-109`); `trackEnds` (`AvailabilityViewModel.kt:399`) and `reloadAfterRestore` (`:778`) launch more. The
read that lands last wins, not the one launched last, so an older snapshot can overwrite a newer one. This PR
adds no read on the Maps tab (the entry map's `getAll()` is a different screen), so production is no likelier
to hit it; the coder's harness hit it by asking twice (`FindPhotosOffMapTest.kt:104-107`, run 40567562398),
and the fix there was to stop asking (`a2bfb4848`). A follow-up, not a known-bug note: a job slot in the
ViewModel cancelled on each new read, the shape the `modular-code` skill names ("every decision to start or
stop a job stays in a ViewModel function", RECORD -1450). If S1 is fixed by a re-read from `onFindDeleted`,
that re-read is one more entrant, so S3 goes first or with it.

**S4. The revert runs' `unit-test-report` artifacts carry stale XML; the job logs are the evidence, not the
artifacts.** Run 40570120331 (r1): the job log says `21 tests completed, 8 failed`, four filter patterns, no
Kotlin `e:` line; the artifact holds nine XML files, 87 tests, 18 failures. The five extra classes
(`OfflineTileBudgetTest`, `AvailabilityViewModelOfflineMapsTest`, two `OfflineMapsPanelText*`,
`RecordDetailsSheetTest`) are timestamped `2026-10-27T04:36Z`, fifteen hours before the run, and their failures
("counts by its estimate, not MapLibre's count of both sources' tiles") are ones a one-line edit to
`GetMapRecordsUseCase` cannot cause. Run 40570137968 (r5): log `21 tests completed, 6 failed`; artifact five
files, one of them `SourcesAndCreditsTest` at `04:36Z`. The four fresh classes in each artifact are timestamped
inside the run and tally exactly with the log. The green run's artifact is clean: 798 files, every timestamp
inside the run, 5,845 tests, 0 failures, 24 skipped, as the PR body says. This is CLAUDE.md's stale-artifact
family; the coder cited the logs, which is right, but the next reader of an artifact alone would count 18.
Cause not determined from here (the stale files sit in `app/build/test-results/testDebugUnitTest/` on a fresh
runner; a Gradle cache restore is the likely route, unconfirmed). Follow-up for the CI workflow, not this PR:
clear `app/build/test-results` before the test step, or upload only what the step produced.

## 3. The harness

No existing test is weakened: the only edits to existing tests add the `ErrorLog` argument
(`GetCartographyEntryMapDataUseCaseTest.kt:62,214`, `TrackDeleteEntryRefsTest.kt:84`,
`NetworkFixExclusionPerConsumerTest.kt`). The wait (`FindPhotosOffMapTest.kt:122-145`) idles the main looper by
hand between checks, the loop `MushroomLogViewModelWiringTest.kt:47-55` already uses; Room's suspend read resumes
on the main looper, which under Robolectric's paused mode runs only when idled, and `composeRule.waitForIdle`
alone did not drain it (the coder's thread dump, run 40560135774). Idling runs work production's looper would run
anyway, so it cannot pass a test the device would fail; it cannot hide a real wait, because the assertion still
reads what the screen handed the map after the read landed. Against CLAUDE.md's stall note: no `runTest`, no
poll loop of the test's own, a real-time deadline with a thread dump on expiry (the note's own diagnostic), and
the database closed in `@After` so a read in flight after a failure errors rather than hangs. The one cost is
that a slow CI box fails the wait instead of hanging, which is the right side to fail on. The 12.9 s first-test
time the coder saw (run 40555150432) was with MapLibre's offline store in the path, since removed
(`RigNoOfflineMaps`, `:97`).

## 7. The S22 steps, as the code says they should read

Not in the PR body, a comment or a commit (N5). From the code:
1. *Find only.* Journal > a saved find with two photos at a location > Maps: one find icon, no camera icon at
   either photo's position (`GetMapRecordsUseCase.kt:104-106`).
2. *Bubble.* Tap the find > its bubble with its cover photo (`MapBubbles.kt:294`) > Back closes the bubble.
3. *Photos switch.* Layers > Photos off: a standalone photo's icon goes, the find's icon stays; on: it returns
   (`FindPhotosOffMapTest.kt:170-187`).
4. *Stack with no fan.* Tap the find with nothing else near it: the bubble opens, no fan.
5. *Fan of find plus standalone photo.* A standalone photo within the stack distance of the find: the fan shows
   the find and that photo, never the find's own photos.
6. *The journal entry case.* An entry shown on the map that attaches a photo of a located find: no icon and no
   ring on Maps; on the entry's own map, no icon (the entry's photo grid still shows the photo, which is not an
   icon).
7. *No location.* A saved find with no location: no find icon, its located photos as camera icons.
8. *Draft then Save.* New find with a photo, not yet saved: the photo's icon shows; Save, then Maps: the find's
   icon, the photo's gone. The Maps tab must be re-entered after Save to reload (the snapshot).
9. *Delete then wait out Undo.* This is S1: the step must say whether the user waits on Maps (photos appear
   only after leaving and re-entering Maps, at `a2bfb4848`) or on the Journal (then Maps: photos back).
