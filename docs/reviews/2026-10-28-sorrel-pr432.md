## Findings

**S (must fix before merge): none.**

**N1. The panel's Back does not yield to the Tools drawer; reachable in landscape only.** `MapTypePanel.kt:80` registers
`BackHandler(onBack = onDismiss)` while the panel is open, with no drawer condition, where the add tile's handler yields
(`AvailabilityCompactMapUi.kt:343`, `enabled = !isDrawerOpen && …`) and the drawer's own handler (`AvailabilityScreenBackHandlers.kt:62`)
is composed above the tab (`AvailabilityScreen.kt:502`), so the panel's, registered later, is asked first. Failure path: landscape,
panel open, tap Tools on the rail (the rail is outside the panel's frame, `mapControlsPadding` at `ScaffoldMapTabCall.kt:81, 190`;
the parameter's doc at `AvailabilityCompactMapUi.kt:150-157`) > the drawer opens over the open panel > Back closes the panel behind
the drawer and the drawer stays > a second Back closes the drawer. "Back retraces the way in" would close the drawer first. In
portrait the bottom nav is under the scrim (the nav is composed before the modal overlays in the same Box,
`CompactMapBottomChrome.kt:145-152`), so the path does not exist there. Not tested; not verified on a device. The fix is a one-case
decision (yield on `isDrawerOpen`, or close the panel when the drawer opens), so it is the planner's, not mine.

**N2. A premise in the verify-first report is wrong: the Worker is in this repository.** Section 3 says the manifest "lives in another
repository and is not touched". `server/pmtiles-worker/src/index.ts:420-427` serves `/reference-trails-manifest.json`, documented at
`server/pmtiles-worker/README.md:57` and tested at `test/worker.test.mjs:139, 632`. With `WorkerReferenceTrailsManifestSource.kt`
deleted, that route has no client in the app. Harmless as it stands (a day's cache, an uncached 404 when the object is missing), and
retiring it is a Worker change that deploys from main on its own (RECORD -1609), so it is the owner's call, outside this PR.

**N3. The two folds in Tools now behave differently.** Map Layers survives a trip into Settings and rotation (`AvailabilityScreen.kt:321`,
`rememberSaveable`); Trip Planner resets on both (`SearchControls.kt:92`, `remember`), and its doc comment at `:63-65` records the
opposite intent to CLAUDE.md's UX default. Hazel reported it (verify report, section 4) and left it, as the dispatch said. For the
owner: one list, two memories.

**N4. Q2's "Option 2" and what was built.** The verify report's Q2 options were A (leave), B (Title Case on the switch only) and C
(everywhere); the owner answered "Option 2. But keep taxonomy proper". Amendment 2 resolves it as Title Case everywhere, and that
is what landed (`ColourFields.kt:47-49, 64`: one `label`, so switch, legend, bubble and spoken labels all follow). If "Option 2"
meant B, the legend and bubble now read Title Case against the owner's intent. Debug-only layers, never listed in a release build
(`MapLayers.kt:372-376`). For Rowan to confirm against whatever restatement the owner answered; I could not.

**N5. Test gap: Back on the Maps tab with fullscreen on and the panel open.** `MapTypePanelTest.kt:139-148` runs with fullscreen off;
the entry-map test does assert fullscreen survives (`:294`). The fullscreen handler at `AvailabilityScreenBackHandlers.kt:65` is
composed before the panel's, so by registration order the panel wins; S22 step 6 is the only check. One more case would close it.

**N6. Fold header rows are about 24 dp tall.** `SearchControls.kt:106-117` has no `heightIn`; the rows the fold holds use
`MIN_TOUCH_TARGET` (`MapLayersToolsSection.kt:148`). Pre-existing, flagged by Hazel, out of scope; noted because the arrow's removal
leaves the header a text-only target.

**N7. Comments in ten files cite the deleted sheet for the `contentColorFor` reasoning** ("see `MapLayersSheet` (removed …)":
`Basemap.kt:39`, `MapMode.kt:6`, `JournalEntriesChip.kt:66`, `MapChrome.kt:91, 109`, `CentrePinLocationPicker.kt:377`,
`SearchNotice.kt:48`, `SpeciesSearchField.kt:173`, `RecordDetailsSheet.kt:123`, `AvailabilityMapOverlaysUi.kt:146, 218`). The reasoning
survives at `MapChrome.kt:103-109` (`mapChromeFill`'s doc); pointing there would spare the next reader a dead reference. Cosmetic.

**N8. `NoDropDownChevronsTest` sees Kotlin icon names only.** `NoDropDownChevronsTest.kt:21` lists seven names; a chevron drawn from a
vector drawable through `painterResource` would pass it. None exists today (my `find` over `res/`), so the claim holds; the doc comment
could say what the check does not cover.

**N9. Trails is grey until the Maps tab's map has reported once.** `ReferenceTrailsShown.shown` starts `false`
(`ReferenceTrailsVisibility.kt:118`). Tools opened before the first style load, or over the Journal in a process where the Maps tab's

choice; noted so nobody files it as a bug. Whether the app can start on the Journal I did not check.
